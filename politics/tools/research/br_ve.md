# Research note — Brazil & Venezuela (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-11. Every fact in `units/br_ve.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Brazil and Venezuela: the Amazon border, Lula and Chávez, Odebrecht and BNDES defaults; the Pacaraima crossing, Operation Welcome and refugee policy; Lula's break with Maduro, Essequibo, BRICS and the 2026 US raid.

## Lula, Chávez and the builders (`br_ve-1`)
- 1859: Border treaty between Brazil and Venezuela
- 1999: Hugo Chávez takes office
- 2003: Lula takes office; the 'pink tide' rises
- 2006: Bridge over the Orinoco, built by Odebrecht, opens
- 2012: Venezuela joins Mercosur with Brazil's backing
- 2017–18: Venezuela defaults on loans from Brazil's BNDES

- Source: [Wikipedia: Brazil–Venezuela relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil%E2%80%93Venezuela_relations)
- Source: [Al Jazeera: Brazil ex-president Lula investigated for corruption](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2015/7/17/brazil-ex-president-lula-investigated-for-corruption)

## The crossing at Pacaraima (`br_ve-2`)
- 2015–16: Venezuelans begin crossing into Roraima
- Feb 2018: Brazil launches Operação Acolhida
- Aug 2018: Locals attack migrant camps in Pacaraima
- 2019: Brazil recognises Venezuelans as refugees en masse
- Mar 2023: 100,000 Venezuelans relocated from Roraima
- 2025: About 96,000 new arrivals in the year
- Source: [IOM Brazil: Operação Acolhida atinge a marca de 100 mil pessoas refugiadas e migrantes venezuelanas interiorizadas](https://brazil.iom.int/pt-br/news/operacao-acolhida-atinge-marca-de-100-mil-pessoas-refugiadas-e-migrantes-venezuelanas-interiorizadas)
- Source: [Global Health NOW: Migration Response Done Right: Brazil's Model for a World in Crisis](https://globalhealthnow.org/2025-07/migration-response-done-right-brazils-model-world-crisis)
- Source: [Refugees International: Latinoamérica21: The World Should Take Note of Brazil's Refugee Policy](https://www.refugeesinternational.org/latinoamerica21-the-world-should-take-note-of-brazils-refugee-policy/)
- Source: [ND Mais: Qual o impacto da crise na Venezuela em Roraima?](https://ndmais.com.br/politica/impacto-crise-venezuela-e-roraima-brasil/)

- Dec 2023: Brazil moves troops to Roraima amid the Essequibo crisis

- Oct 2024: Brazil blocks Venezuela's bid to become a BRICS partner
- 3 Jan 2026: US forces seize Maduro; Lula condemns the raid
