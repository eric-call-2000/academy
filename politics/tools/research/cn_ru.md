# Research note — China & Russia (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/cn_ru.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: From Stalin's alliance and a 1969 border war to a 'no limits' partnership; China's role in Russia's war economy; and an unequal bargain of oil, gas and cars. The Ukraine war itself is in ru_ua and ru-6.

## Comrades, enemies, partners (`cn_ru-1`)
- 1950: Mao and Stalin sign a treaty of alliance
- Late 1950s: The Sino-Soviet split begins
- 2 Mar 1969: Clash on Zhenbao Island; months of border fighting follow
- 1972: Nixon visits China, exploiting the split
- 1989: Gorbachev visits Beijing; relations normalised
- 2001: Treaty of friendship
- 2004–08: The border is finally settled
- Source: [Hoover Institution: The 1969 Sino-Soviet Border Conflicts As A Key Turning Point Of The Cold War](https://www.hoover.org/research/1969-sino-soviet-border-conflicts-key-turning-point-cold-war)
- Source: [The China Project: China, Russia, and the legacy of Zhenbao Island](https://thechinaproject.com/2020/03/03/china-russia-and-the-legacy-of-zhenbao-island/)
- Source: [Meduza: Two countries, one island: Russia and China divided up an island in the Far East in 2004](https://meduza.io/en/feature/2018/11/02/two-countries-one-island)

## 'No limits' (`cn_ru-2`)
- 4 Feb 2022: 'No limits' joint statement in Beijing
- 24 Feb 2022: Russia invades Ukraine; China refuses to condemn it
- Mar 2023: Xi visits Moscow
- May 2025: Xi attends Moscow's Victory Day parade
- Sep 2025: Putin at Beijing's military parade
- May 2026: Putin in Beijing: over 40 agreements signed
- Source: [US-China Economic and Security Review Commission: China's Position on Russia's Invasion of Ukraine](https://www.uscc.gov/research/chinas-position-russias-invasion-ukraine)
- Source: [CNBC: Three key takeaways from Putin's Beijing trip — and what they reveal about China-Russia ties](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/21/china-russia-putin-xi-jinping-ties-deals-energy-siberia-pipeline-trump-visits-.html)
- Source: [Scripps News via WKBW: Chinese dual-use electronics are helping Iran and Russia build deadlier drones, despite US sanctions](https://www.wkbw.com/world/asia/chinese-dual-use-electronics-are-helping-iran-and-russia-build-deadlier-drones-despite-u-s-sanctions)
- Source: [MERICS: China-Russia Dashboard: Facts and figures on a special relationship](https://merics.org/en/china-russia-dashboard-facts-and-figures-special-relationship)

## An unequal bargain (`cn_ru-3`)
- 2024: A record $243.6 billion, by Chinese customs figures
- 2025: About $234 billion, the first fall in five years
- Oil: China buys about half of Russia's crude exports
- Gas: Power of Siberia 1, opened December 2019
- Cars: Chinese brands dominate Russia's new-car market
