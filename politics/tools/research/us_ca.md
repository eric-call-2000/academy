## Allies (briefing 1)
- Border 8,891 km (longest between two countries). Ogdensburg Agreement Aug 1940 (Permanent Joint Board on Defence). NORAD 1958; deputy commander Canadian. 158 Canadian military deaths in Afghanistan. Canada stayed out of Iraq 2003.
- 9/11: Operation Yellow Ribbon — more than 200 diverted flights; Gander took ~6,600–7,000 passengers. (Sources differ on exact totals; keep the text approximate.)
- NORAD modernisation C$38.6bn over 20 years (2022). Trump's Golden Dome price for Canada: $61bn (May 2025), later $71bn. — [CCPA](https://www.policyalternatives.ca/news-research/fact-sheet-golden-dome-and-canada/), [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/north-american-aerospace-defense-command-norad-modernization), [Small Wars Journal](https://smallwarsjournal.com/2026/03/04/norad-us-canada-tensions/)

## Softwood lumber (briefing 2)
- Lumber I 1982; Lumber II MOU 1986 (15% Canadian export charge); 2006 SLA refunded ~$4–4.5bn, expired 12 Oct 2015; Lumber V since. — [CRS R44851](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R44851), [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/softwood-lumber-dispute)
- AD/CVD combined ~35% from Aug 2025 (AR6); Section 232 10% on softwood lumber from Oct 2025 (cabinets/vanities 25% → 50% on 1 Jan 2026); AR7 preliminary (2026) would cut the combined AD/CVD rate to ~24.8%, finals Aug–Oct 2026. — [NAHB Aug 2025](https://www.nahb.org/blog/2025/08/canadian-lumber-cvd-rates), [NAHB Apr 2026](https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/04/canadian-lumbers-duties-to-drop), [Global Affairs Canada](https://www.international.gc.ca/controls-controles/softwood-bois_oeuvre/recent.aspx?lang=eng)
- Canada supplies roughly a quarter of US softwood lumber consumption; C$1.2bn federal support package Aug 2025. — [CRS R48781](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48781)

## Energy and water (briefing 3)
- 2025: Canada **63.4%** of US crude imports; crude + products ~4.5 mb/d; electricity exports **32.7 TWh**, ~$3.3bn. — [CER](https://www.cer-rec.gc.ca/en/data-analysis/energy-markets/market-snapshots/2026/market-snapshot-overview-of-2025-canada-us-energy-trade.html), [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67924)
- Keystone XL: rejected 2015, approved 2017, permit revoked 20 Jan 2021. Trans Mountain expansion in service May 2024. Carney–Alberta pipeline MOU Nov 2025 (ca-4).
- Ontario 25% electricity surcharge 10 Mar 2025, suspended 11 Mar after Trump's threat of 50% steel/aluminium tariffs. — [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/us-canada-trade-war-threatens-electricity-imports-prices/828689/)
- Boundary Waters Treaty 1909; Columbia River Treaty 1964, modernisation agreed in principle July 2024, paused by the US in 2025.
- March 2025 IEEPA tariffs: 25% on non-USMCA goods, 10% on energy and potash.
