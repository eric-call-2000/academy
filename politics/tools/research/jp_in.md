# Research note — Japan & India (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/jp_in.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Japan and India: Bose, Justice Pal and old goodwill; Suzuki, the Delhi Metro and the bullet train; Abe's two seas, the Quad and Takaichi's visit.

## Bose, Pal and old goodwill (`jp_in-1`)
- 6th century: Buddhism, born in India, reaches Japan
- 21 Oct 1943: Bose proclaims a provisional Free India government with Japanese backing
- 1944: Japanese and INA forces fail at Imphal and Kohima
- 1948: Justice Pal dissents at the Tokyo tribunal
- 1952: India signs a separate peace treaty with Japan, waiving reparations
- 1958: Japan's first yen loan goes to India
- Source: [European Journal of International Relations: The trials of imperialism: Radhabinod Pal's dissent at the Tokyo tribunal](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1354066114555775)
- Source: [Encyclopaedia Britannica: Subhas Chandra Bose](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Subhas-Chandra-Bose)
- Source: [The Geopolitics: Remembering Radhabinod Pal's Dissenting Opinion at the Tokyo Trial](https://thegeopolitics.com/remembering-radhabinod-pals-dissenting-opinion-at-the-tokyo-trial/)

## Suzuki, the metro and the bullet train (`jp_in-2`)
- Maruti Suzuki: Joint venture from 1982; first car in 1983
- Aid: Japan is India's largest bilateral development lender
- Delhi Metro: Built largely with Japanese loans; opened 2002
- Bullet train: Mumbai–Ahmedabad, 508 km, first section due 2027
- Aug 2025: Target of ¥10 trillion (~$68 billion) investment over ten years
- Source: [Nikkei Asia: Japan to invest $68bn in India over 10 years, including AI and chips](https://asia.nikkei.com/politics/international-relations/japan-to-invest-68bn-in-india-over-10-years-including-ai-and-chips)
- Source: [Australian Institute of International Affairs: Modi–Ishiba Summit Seeks Deeper India–Japan Ties Amid Uncertain Geopolitics](https://www.internationalaffairs.org.au/australianoutlook/modi-ishiba-summit-seeks-deeper-india-japan-ties-amid-uncertain-geopolitics/)

## Two seas and the Quad (`jp_in-3`)
- 22 Aug 2007: Abe's 'Confluence of the Two Seas' speech in Delhi
- 2007: First Quad meeting of Japan, India, the US and Australia
- 2014: 'Special Strategic and Global Partnership'
- 2017: The Quad revived
- 2020: Japan becomes a permanent member of the Malabar naval exercise
- Jul 2026: Takaichi's first visit to India as prime minister
