# Research note — France & Nigeria (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-12. Every fact in `units/fr_ng.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: France and Nigeria: the 1961 break over Sahara bomb tests, French arms for Biafra, MSF and ECOWAS; TotalEnergies, Macron's Abuja internship and Lagos visit, the Sahel withdrawals and the Niger coup; Tinubu's 2024 state visit, security help and the September 2026 Élysée dinner.

## A bomb, Biafra and suspicion (`fr_ng-1`)
- 1960: Nigeria becomes independent from Britain
- Feb 1960: France tests its first atomic bomb in the Algerian Sahara
- Jan 1961: Nigeria breaks diplomatic relations with France
- 1967–70: Nigerian civil war; France covertly arms Biafra
- 1971: French doctors back from Biafra found Médecins Sans Frontières
- 1975: ECOWAS founded in Lagos
- Source: [Wikipedia: France–Nigeria relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France%E2%80%93Nigeria_relations)
- Source: [Britannica: Nigerian Civil War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Nigerian-civil-war)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Médecins Sans Frontières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9decins_Sans_Fronti%C3%A8res)

## Oil, Lagos and the Niger coup (`fr_ng-2`)
- 1960s: Elf and later Total begin producing oil in Nigeria
- 2002: Macron interns at the French embassy in Abuja
- 2018: Egina deep-water field starts production
- Jul 2018: Macron visits Lagos and Fela Kuti's Afrika Shrine
- Jul 2023: Coup in Niger; ECOWAS, chaired by Tinubu, threatens force
- Jan 2025: Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso leave ECOWAS
