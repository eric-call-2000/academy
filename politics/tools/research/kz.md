## Constitution and elections
- Referendum 15 Mar 2026: new constitution, 87.15% yes, turnout 73.12%; in force 1 Jul 2026; single-chamber Kurultai of 145, proportional, five-year term; vice-presidency restored; Kurultai consents to key appointments. — [CEVRO](https://www.cevro.cz/en/caps/kazakhstan-adopts-new-constitution-after-march-2026-referendum), [Verfassungsblog](https://verfassungsblog.de/kazakhstan-constitution/)
- Kurultai election 23 Aug 2026: Adilet 71.17%, Auyl 6.26%, Respublica 5.56%, Ak Zhol 5.21%, OSDP 5.11%; turnout 74.08%; preliminary seats Adilet ~110 of 145; OSCE: lack of distinct opposition; court ruling would let Tokayev run again in 2029; inflation 10.2% in February. — [Dunyo](https://dunyo.info/en/news/v-kazahstane-ozvuchili-predvaritelnye-itogi-vyborov-v-kurultay), [NY1/AP](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2026/08/24/kazakhstans-pro-government-party-wins-parliamentary-election-boosting-presidents-grip-on-power), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Kazakh_legislative_election)
- 31 Aug 2026: Erlan Karin vice-president; Olzhas Bektenov reappointed PM. — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/31/tokayevs-former-state-adviser-erlan-karin-appointed-kazakhstans-vice-president), [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/31/kazakhstans-president-taps-longtime-adviser-for-vice-president-role)

## History (standard references)
- Kazakh Khanate c. 1465; Dzungar invasion 1723; Junior Horde 1731; Kenesary 1847; 1916 revolt; Alash Orda 1917–20; famine 1930–33 (~1.5m, 38–40%); Semipalatinsk 456 tests 1949–89, closed 29 Aug 1991; Nevada–Semipalatinsk 1989; Virgin Lands 1954; Jeltoqsan Dec 1986; independence 16 Dec 1991; ~1,400 warheads removed by 1995; Astana 1997; Zhanaozen 2011; Nazarbayev resigned 2019; Jan 2022 unrest (238 killed, ~10,000 detained, CSTO); Massimov 18 years; 2022 referendum: single 7-year term; Constitutional Court 2023. — [Britannica: history of Kazakhstan](https://www.britannica.com/topic/history-of-Kazakhstan), [Wikipedia: famine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_famine_of_1930%E2%80%931933), [Wikipedia: Semipalatinsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semipalatinsk_Test_Site), [Wikipedia: 2022 unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Kazakh_unrest)
