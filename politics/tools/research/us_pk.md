# Research note — United States & Pakistan (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-01. Every fact in `units/us_pk.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and Pakistan: SEATO, the U-2 from Peshawar, 1965, Kissinger's secret flight and the 1971 tilt; the Afghan jihad, Pressler sanctions, A. Q. Khan, 9/11, Abbottabad and the 2018 aid cut; Trump, Munir, the 2025 trade and minerals deals and the Iran mediation. The mediation is in pk-6.

## Cold War allies of convenience (`us_pk-1`)
- 1954: Mutual defence agreement; Pakistan joins SEATO
- 1955: Pakistan joins the Baghdad Pact (later CENTO)
- May 1960: A U-2 flying from Peshawar is shot down over the USSR
- 1965: US cuts off arms to both Pakistan and India during their war
- Jul 1971: Kissinger flies secretly to Beijing from Pakistan
- Dec 1971: Nixon 'tilts' toward Pakistan; Bangladesh is born

- 1998: Nuclear tests bring new sanctions
- 2001: After 9/11, Musharraf joins the US 'war on terror'
