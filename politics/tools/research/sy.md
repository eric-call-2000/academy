## The transition
- 8 Dec 2024: Assad flees to Moscow after an 11-day offensive led by Hayat Tahrir al-Sham (HTS). Sharaa named president 29 Jan 2025 (HTS and other factions dissolved). Constitutional Declaration 13 Mar 2025: five-year transition; strong presidency; Islamic jurisprudence "the main source" of legislation. People's Assembly: 210 seats; two-thirds chosen by regional electoral colleges in Oct 2025, one-third named by Sharaa on 1 Jul 2026; first sitting July 2026; speaker Abdel Halim al-Awak; no confidence vote over the government; 30-month renewable term; permanent constitution expected by 2029. — [Wikipedia: Constitutional Declaration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Declaration_of_the_Syrian_Arab_Republic), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/12/syrian-parliament-convenes-for-first-time-following-al-assads-overthrow), [ConstitutionNet](https://constitutionnet.org/news/new-syrian-parliament-convenes), [House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10430/), [Wikipedia: Presidency of Ahmed al-Sharaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Ahmed_al-Sharaa)

## Violence
- Coast, March 2025: about 1,400 killed, mostly Alawite civilians (UN commission, Aug 2025: war crimes likely by both government forces and Assad loyalists); government committee 1,426 dead, 298 suspects, no evidence commanders ordered it; Reuters counted 1,479 Alawites killed at 40 sites; SOHR more than 1,700. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/8/14/war-crimes-likely-committed-by-both-sides-in-syria-sectarian-violence-un-commission-says), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/7/22/more-than-1400-killed-in-sectarian-violence-in-coastal-syria-report-finds), [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/over-1400-mostly-civilians-were-killed-in-sectarian-violence-in-coastal-syria-in-march-government-says)
- Sweida, July 2025: clashes between Druze groups and Bedouin clans; government forces accused of killing Druze civilians; hundreds killed; more than 128,500 displaced (UN); Israel struck government forces, saying it was protecting the Druze; US-brokered ceasefire; Jordan- and US-backed roadmap. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/israel-and-syria-agree-on-ceasefire-after-days-of-bloodshed-in-sweida-says-us-envoy?amp=1), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/09/exclusive-israel-syria-talks-hit-snag-over-humanitarian-corridor-sources-say)

## The northeast
- Jan 2026: after clashes over an earlier (March 2025) deal, the SDF left Aleppo neighbourhoods; government push toward Raqqa; ceasefire signed 18 Jan 2026: handover of Raqqa and Deir ez-Zor, integration of SDF fighters, state takeover of border crossings, oil and gas fields and IS detainee camps, SDF nominees in senior posts; integration agreement 29 Jan. Aug 2026: Sharaa and Mazloum Abdi meet; process in "final stages". — [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/01/erdogan-welcomes-syria-sdf-agreement-urges-full-implementation), [Enab Baladi](https://english.enabbaladi.net/archives/2026/01/the-syrian-army-completes-its-takeover-of-raqqa-and-deir-ezzor/), [Enab Baladi](https://english.enabbaladi.net/archives/2026/08/al-sharaa-abdi-discuss-sdf-integration-agreement/)

## Israel
- Dec 2024: Israel seized the UN buffer zone and the Syrian side of Mount Hermon. Sept 2025: talks on a de-escalation pact stalled over an Israeli demand for an aid corridor to Sweida. 6 Jan 2026 (Paris): US-supervised joint "fusion mechanism" for intelligence sharing and de-escalation. Talks paused after the Iran war began in Feb 2026. Oct 2026: Netanyahu says he held a call with Sharaa, the first disclosed. — [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20260106-israel-syria-intelligence-military-de-escalation-paris), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/6/israel-syria-agree-to-dedicated-communication-line-in-us-brokered-talks), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/first-israels-netanyahu-says-he-held-call-syrias-ahmed-al-sharaa), [Wikipedia: Israel–Syria relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Syria_relations)

World Bank reconstruction estimate $216bn. — [KUOW/NPR](https://www.kuow.org/stories/syrian-president-sharaa-makes-the-1st-white-house-visit-by-a-syrian-head-of-state), [Annahar](https://fr.annahar.com/en/region/middle-east/263688/syria-welcomes-the-permanent-repeal-of-sweeping-us-sanctions), [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/125619/removing-syria-state-sponsor-terrorism-designation/)

## History (standard references)
- Damascus among the oldest cities; Umayyad caliphate 661–750; Ottoman rule 1516–1918; Faisal's Arab Kingdom 1920; French mandate; independence 1946; coups from 1949; UAR with Egypt 1958–61; Ba'ath coup 1963; Hafez al-Assad 1970–2000; 1973 war; Hama, Feb 1982 (estimates 10,000–40,000 killed); Bashar from 2000; 2011 uprising; civil war deaths estimated at about half a million or more; more than 13 million displaced; Ghouta sarin attack 2013; IS caliphate 2014–19; Russian intervention 2015; 2023 earthquake. — [Britannica: Syria](https://www.britannica.com/place/Syria), [Britannica: Syrian Civil War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Syrian-Civil-War), [Wikipedia: 1982 Hama massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Hama_massacre), [Wikipedia: Fall of the Assad regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_the_Assad_regime)

June 2025 Damascus church bombing, blamed by the government on IS. Captagon stocks seized after Dec 2024. Cabinet of March 2025 includes a Christian woman, a Kurd, a Druze and an Alawite; no prime minister. — [Wikipedia: Presidency of Ahmed al-Sharaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Ahmed_al-Sharaa), [Britannica: Syria](https://www.britannica.com/place/Syria)
