- 1996: Last nuclear warheads leave Ukraine
- Feb–Mar 2014: Russia seizes and annexes Crimea
- 2015–22: Britain's Operation Orbital trains Ukrainian troops
- Jan 2022: Britain flies anti-tank weapons to Kyiv
