# Research note — Canada & Mexico (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-11. Every fact in `units/ca_mx.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Canada and Mexico: diplomatic relations, the seasonal worker programme, NAFTA, mining and tourism; the 2009, 2016 and 2024 visa decisions and asylum claims; Trump's tariffs, the 2025 action plan and the 2026 USMCA review.

## Neighbours of a neighbour (`ca_mx-1`)
- 1944: Canada and Mexico establish diplomatic relations
- 1974: Mexican workers join Canada's seasonal farm worker programme
- 1990: Canada joins the Organization of American States
- 1 Jan 1994: NAFTA takes effect
- 2004: Canada–Mexico Partnership launched
- 2010s: Millions of Canadians holiday in Mexico each year

## Visas and asylum (`ca_mx-2`)
- 2009: Harper government imposes visas on Mexicans
- Jun 2016: Trudeau promises to lift the visa
- 1 Dec 2016: Visa requirement lifted
- 2023: Nearly 24,000 asylum claims by Mexicans
- 29 Feb 2024: Visa reimposed for most Mexican travellers
- Mar–Sep 2024: Mexican asylum claims fall by 70%
- Source: [Seattle Times (AP): Canada reimposes a visa requirement on Mexicans](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/canada-reimposes-a-visa-requirement-on-mexicans/)
- Source: [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada: Mexico: Border Crossings/Asylum Claims (CIMM briefing)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/transparency/committees/cimm-nov-25-2024/mexico-border-crossings-asylum-claims.html)
- Source: [BDO Canada: Visa requirements reimposed for Mexican nationals](https://www.bdo.ca/insights/canada-reimposes-visa-requirements-for-mexican-nationals)
