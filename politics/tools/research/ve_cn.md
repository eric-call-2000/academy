# Research note — Venezuela & China (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/ve_cn.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Venezuela and China: Chávez's courtship, satellites and weapons; oil-for-loans and arrears; the 2026 raid and the debt question. The raid is in ve-5, US oil deals in ve-6.

## Chávez's new friend (`ve_cn-1`)
- 1999: Chávez visits Beijing in his first year in office
- 2001: Jiang Zemin visits Caracas; a high-level commission is set up
- 2007: The first Chinese oil-backed loan fund
- 2008: Venezuela orders Chinese K-8 jets; China launches its first satellite
- 2012: China launches Venezuela's first Earth-observation satellite
- 2013–14: Chinese armoured vehicles bought, and used against protests

## Oil for loans (`ve_cn-2`)
- 2007: China Development Bank begins oil-backed lending
- 2007–15: Loan commitments grow to about $60 billion in 17 contracts
- 2014: Oil prices crash
- 2016: Venezuela starts falling behind on payments
- 2019: US sanctions on Venezuela's oil industry
- 2025: China takes about three-quarters of Venezuela's oil exports
- Source: [AidData: How China's oil-backed lending in Venezuela fell into distress—and what might come next](https://www.aiddata.org/blog/how-chinas-oil-backed-lending-in-venezuela-fell-into-distress)
- Source: [US-China Economic and Security Review Commission: China-Venezuela Fact Sheet: A Short Primer on the Relationship](https://www.uscc.gov/research/china-venezuela-fact-sheet-short-primer-relationship)
- Source: [Center on Global Energy Policy, Columbia University: US Action Threatens Venezuela-China Oil Flows, Debt Repayment, and Investments](https://www.energypolicy.columbia.edu/venezuela-china-oil-ties-severely-impacted-by-us-action/)
