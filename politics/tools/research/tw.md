## Key facts
- Lai Ching-te president since May 2024; KMT–TPP opposition controls the Legislative Yuan.
- Great Recall votes (2025) failed to unseat any KMT legislator.
- Special defence budget cut to NT$780bn; drone programme.
- US–Taiwan trade deal Jan 2026; Trump–Xi summit 24 Sep 2026.
- Local elections 28 Nov 2026.
