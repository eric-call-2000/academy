Checked 2026-10-12. Every fact in `units/sa_ru.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).

## The Red Pasha and a long freeze (`sa_ru-1`)
- Feb 1926: The USSR recognises Ibn Saud as king of the Hejaz
- 1932: Prince Faisal visits Moscow
- 1938: Soviet envoy Karim Khakimov is executed; the mission closes
- 1979–89: Soviet war in Afghanistan; Saudis fund the mujahideen
- Sep 1990: Diplomatic relations restored
- 2003: Crown Prince Abdullah visits Moscow

## OPEC+ and a price war (`sa_ru-2`)
- Feb 2007: Putin is the first Russian leader to visit Riyadh
- 2014: Oil price crash
- Dec 2016: OPEC+ agreement on output cuts
- Oct 2017: King Salman visits Moscow
- Mar 2020: Talks collapse; a price war begins
- Apr 2020: Record 9.7 million barrel a day cut
