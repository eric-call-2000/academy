## Russia's forward base
- Oreshnik: Russian MoD confirmed deployment in Belarus end Dec 2025, reportedly at the former Krichev airfield; Lukashenko says up to 10; Russian control. Lukashenko claims dozens of Russian tactical nuclear weapons, deployment announced 2023; monitors say no independent evidence of their presence. 2022 referendum dropped non-nuclear status. — [Novaya Gazeta Europe](https://novayagazeta.eu/en/articles/2025/12/30/russia-deploys-oreshnik-missile-system-in-belarus-en-news), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/europe/russia-s-nuclear-capable-oreshnik-missiles-have-entered-active-service-moscow-says-1.500394259), [iSANS](https://isans.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/belarusian-nuclear-myth-2.pdf)

## The border
- Lithuania closed the Šalčininkai and Medininkai crossings in Oct–Nov 2025 over smuggling balloons ("a hybrid attack"); Belarus held Lithuanian trucks until end of March 2026; Vilnius airport closed repeatedly; further closures threatened in 2026. — [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/europe/balloons-belarus-disrupt-lithuanian-airport-rcna239833), [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/08/02/lithuania-closes-border-with-belarus-over-repeated-balloon-incursions), [KJZZ/NPR](https://www.kjzz.org/npr-top-stories/2025-12-03/belarus-weather-balloons-force-repeated-closures-of-lithuanias-main-airport)
- Poland: Kuźnica closed Nov 2021 (migrants), Bobrowniki Feb 2023 (Poczobut sentence); border sealed during Zapad-2025; both reopened 16–17 Nov 2025; 536,000 people crossed in six months. — [Polskie Radio](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7785/Artykul/3608375,poland-reopens-two-key-border-crossings-with-belarus), [Reform.news](https://reform.news/en/half-a-million-people-crossed-the-border-at-kuznica-and-bobrowniki-in-six-months/amp)

## History and system (standard references)
- Polotsk principality; Grand Duchy of Lithuania; Commonwealth; partitions 1772–95; Belarusian People's Republic 25 Mar 1918; BSSR 1919; western Belarus annexed 1939; WWII: roughly a quarter of the population killed, Khatyn (22 Mar 1943, 149 villagers), hundreds of villages burned with their people; Holocaust (Minsk ghetto); Chernobyl 1986 (about 70% of fallout on Belarus, about a fifth of the land contaminated); independence 1991, Belavezha Accords Dec 1991; Lukashenko elected 1994; 1996 referendum; Union State treaty 1999; 2010 crackdown; 2020 election (official 80%), mass protests; Ryanair diversion May 2021; 2021 migrant crisis; 2022 constitution (All-Belarusian People's Assembly; non-nuclear status dropped); 2022 invasion route to Kyiv; Prigozhin exile after June 2023 mutiny. — [Britannica: Belarus](https://www.britannica.com/place/Belarus), [Wikipedia: Khatyn massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khatyn_massacre), [IAEA: Chernobyl](https://www.iaea.org/topics/chornobyl), [Wikipedia: 2020–2021 protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_Belarusian_protests), [Wikipedia: 2022 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Belarusian_constitutional_referendum)
