## Key facts
- 2024 election: ANC 40.2%, MK 14.6%; GNU of ten parties. Local elections 4 Nov 2026.
- Hill-Lewis DA leader 12 Apr 2026 (>90%).
- US: aid cut Feb 2025; Rasool expelled Mar 2025; Afrikaner refugees; Oval Office 21 May 2025; 30% tariff; G20 Johannesburg boycotted Nov 2025; excluded from 2026 G20; left off G7 guest list Mar 2026.
- Mkhwanazi allegations 6 Jul 2025; Madlanga Commission: prima facie evidence against 14 officials; 13 officers charged by Jul 2026 (Daily Maverick); ad hoc committee preliminary findings Aug 2026.
- Polls Sep 2026: ANC 31, DA 25, MK 14, EFF 11 (one survey); Johannesburg ANC 31, DA 27–28, ActionSA 14; Tshwane DA 31, ANC 22; eThekwini MK ~55.
- Removed from FATF grey list Oct 2025.
