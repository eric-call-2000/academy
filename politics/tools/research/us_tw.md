# Research note — United States & Taiwan (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/us_tw.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and Taiwan: treaty ally to unofficial ties under the Taiwan Relations Act, strategic ambiguity and arms sales, and Trump's transactional approach after the 2026 Beijing summit. Chips and tariffs are in tw-6; drills in tw-7.

## From treaty ally to unofficial friend (`us_tw-1`)
- Jun 1950: Korean War: the US Seventh Fleet moves into the Taiwan Strait
- Dec 1954: US–Republic of China Mutual Defense Treaty
- Feb 1972: Nixon in China; the Shanghai Communiqué
- 1 Jan 1979: The US recognises Beijing and cuts ties with Taipei
- 10 Apr 1979: Carter signs the Taiwan Relations Act
- 1982: Reagan's Six Assurances to Taiwan
- Source: [Congressional Research Service: Taiwan: Background and U.S. Relations](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF10275)
- Source: [Congressional Research Service: President Reagan's Six Assurances to Taiwan](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/PDF/IF11665/IF11665.9.pdf)
- Source: [US Code (House of Representatives): 22 USC Ch. 48: Taiwan Relations](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=%2Fprelim%40title22%2Fchapter48&edition=prelim)

## Strategic ambiguity and the porcupine (`us_tw-2`)
- 1995–96: China fires missiles near Taiwan; the US sends two carrier groups
- Biden, 2021–22: Said four times the US would defend Taiwan
- Biden's sales: 19 rounds, about $8.4 billion over four years
- 17 Dec 2025: Trump notifies a record $11.1 billion in sales
- Main items: HIMARS rockets, howitzers, Javelin and TOW missiles, drones
- Source: [Al Jazeera: US approves $11bn in arms sales to Taiwan in deal likely to anger China](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/18/us-approves-11bn-in-arms-sales-to-taiwan-in-deal-likely-to-anger-china)
- Source: [Prospect Foundation: Feeding the Porcupine: The U.S. December 2025 Arms Sales to Taiwan](https://www.pf.org.tw/en/pfen/33-11787.html)
- Source: [Global Taiwan Institute: The Taiwan Relations Act at 45: Incremental Clarity of Intent](https://globaltaiwan.org/2024/04/the-taiwan-relations-act-at-45-incremental-clarity-of-intent/)
- Source: [Foreign Policy Research Institute: Taiwan Relations Act in a New Era of Security Cooperation](https://www.fpri.org/article/2025/08/taiwan-relations-act-in-a-new-era-of-security-cooperation/)

## A bargaining chip? (`us_tw-3`)
- Jul–Aug 2025: Lai's New York stopover refused; he cancels his trip
- 17 Dec 2025: Record $11.1 billion arms sales notified

- Jan 2026: A further $14 billion package is announced
