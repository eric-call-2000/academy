# Research note — United States & Poland (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-07. Every fact in `units/us_pl.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and Poland: Kościuszko, Pulaski, emigration, Wilson's thirteenth point and Yalta; Solidarity, the 1989 transition, NATO accession, Iraq and the Stare Kiejkuty black site; missile defence, US troops, the May 2026 scare and Fort Trump.

## Heroes, emigrants and Yalta (`us_pl-1`)
- 1777: Kościuszko's fortifications help win at Saratoga
- 1779: Pulaski killed leading cavalry at Savannah
- 1870–1914: Around two million Poles emigrate to the US
- Jan 1918: Wilson's Fourteen Points call for an independent Poland
- Feb 1945: Yalta leaves Poland in the Soviet sphere
- 1956–70s: Polish Americans press Washington against communism
- Source: [Encyclopedia.com: Polish Americans](https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/united-states-and-canada/us-history/polish-americans)
- Source: [US Department of State: U.S. Relations With Poland](https://2017-2021.state.gov/u-s-relations-with-poland/index.html)
- Source: [University of Chicago (Chicago Journal of History): Franklin Roosevelt, Polish-Americans, Yalta, and the 1944 election](https://cjh.uchicago.edu/issues/fall16/7.10.pdf)

## Solidarity, NATO and Iraq (`us_pl-2`)
- Aug 1980: Solidarity founded at the Gdańsk shipyard
- Dec 1981: Martial law; the US imposes sanctions
- 1989: Round-table talks and partly free elections
- 12 Mar 1999: Poland joins NATO
- 2003: Polish special forces join the invasion of Iraq
- 2002–05: CIA runs a secret prison at Stare Kiejkuty
- Source: [NATO: Poland and NATO](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/nato-history/history-by-theme/my-country-and-nato/poland-and-nato)
- Source: [NPR: Polish troops join U.S.-led Iraq force](https://www.npr.org/2003/08/09/1390763/polish-troops-join-u-s-led-iraq-force)
- Source: [NPR: European court rules against Poland in CIA black sites case](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2014/07/24/334867581/european-court-rules-against-poland-in-cia-black-sites-case)

## Troops, missiles and 'Fort Trump' (`us_pl-3`)
- Sep 2009: Obama scraps Bush's missile-shield plan
- 2018: President Duda proposes a permanent 'Fort Trump'
- 2020: US troop presence agreed at about 5,500
- Jul 2024: Aegis Ashore missile-defence site at Redzikowo enters service
- May 2026: A US troop rotation is cancelled, then restored
- Sep 2026: Trump and Nawrocki announce progress on a permanent base
