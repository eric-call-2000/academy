## Summits (briefing 1)
- Sunshine Policy 1998; summits Jun 2000, Oct 2007, 2018 (27 Apr, 26 May, Sep). ~$500m secret payment before 2000 summit. — [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Sunshine-Policy)
- Cheonan Mar 2010 (46 dead), Yeonpyeong Nov 2010 (4 dead). 21 reunion rounds, last Aug 2018; 75% of registered separated families have died. — [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/01/15/separated-familes-north-korea-75-percent-have-died-unification-ministry/1171768462506/)
- Liaison office opened 14 Sep 2018, blown up 16 Jun 2020. — [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2020/06/north-korea-demolishes-inter-korean-liaison-office-in-kaesong/); court award ₩44.6bn, Sep 2026. — [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/foreignaffairs/20260916/court-orders-nk-to-pay-s-korea-325-mil-over-2020-demolition-of-liaison-office)

## Kaesong (briefing 2)
- Opened 2004; 124 firms, ~54,700 workers; five-month shutdown Apr–Sep 2013; closed by Seoul 10 Feb 2016. — [38 North](https://www.38north.org/2024/09/kaesong-industrial-complex-a-tortured-history-and-uncertain-future/), [KEI](https://keia.org/analysis/when-will-kaesong-reopen/)
- Roads/rail blown up Oct 2024; 2026 constitution territorial clause. — [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/05/06/North-Korea-constitution-revision-drops-reunification-Kim-Jong-Un/7001778061562/)

## Balloons and loudspeakers (briefing 3)
- Leaflet ban law Dec 2020, struck down Sep 2023. Trash balloons from May 2024; 5,500+ by Sep 2024. — [NK News](https://www.nknews.org/2024/09/north-korea-launches-some-420-trash-balloons-toward-south-first-in-nearly-month/)
- Speakers resumed Jun 2024 — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/06/09/g-s1-3621/south-korea-to-restart-loudspeaker-broadcasts-into-north-to-combat-trash-balloons); halted 11 Jun 2025 — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/06/18/g-s1-73286/battle-of-sounds-inter-korean-border); dismantled Aug 2025 — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/08/09/asia/north-korea-removes-speakers-border-intl-hnk)
- Seoul's "peace first" two-state policy. — [38 North](https://www.38north.org/2026/09/peace-first-seouls-two-state-answer-to-a-changed-peninsula/)
