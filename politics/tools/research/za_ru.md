## Comrades in the struggle (`za_ru-1`)
- 1961: The ANC launches its armed wing, Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK)
- 1963: The Soviet Union becomes MK's biggest arms supplier
- 1976–91: About 200 Soviet military instructors serve in MK camps in Angola
- 1982: About 90% of MK's equipment is Soviet-made
- 1990: Mandela freed; apartheid begins to end
- 1992: South Africa and Russia establish diplomatic relations
