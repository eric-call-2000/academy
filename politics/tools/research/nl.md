## Election and government
- 29 Oct 2025 election (official result 7 Nov): D66 26 seats (+17, ~16.9%), PVV 26 (−11, ~16.7%), VVD 22, GL–PvdA 20 (Timmermans resigned on election night), CDA 18 (+13), JA21 9, FvD 7, BBB 4, NSC 0 (from 19). D66 ahead of PVV by about 29,700 votes. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Dutch_general_election), [NL Times](https://nltimes.nl/2025/11/07/official-election-results-confirm-26-seats-d66-pvv-change-final-exit-polls), [DutchReview](https://dutchreview.com/news/things-to-know-dutch-election-results-2025/)
- Schoof cabinet fell in June 2025 when the PVV quit over asylum. Jetten cabinet sworn in 23 Feb 2026, 117 days after the vote: D66, VVD, CDA; 66 of 150 seats, 22 of 75 in the Senate; the first genuine minority cabinet since 1945. Jetten, 38, youngest and first openly gay Dutch PM. Deputy PMs Dilan Yeşilgöz (VVD, defence; NATO 3.5% core target) and Bart van den Brink (CDA, asylum and migration); Tom Berendsen (CDA) foreign affairs; Eelco Heinen (VVD) finance. — [Wikipedia: Jetten cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jetten_cabinet), [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/02/rob-jettens-team-who-is-who-in-the-new-dutch-cabinet), [Eunews](https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/02/23/liberal-rob-jetten-sworn-in-as-prime-minister-of-the-netherlands/), [HSS](https://europe.hss.de/en/news-global/jetten-i-a-new-minority-government-in-the-netherlands-news13782/)
- Coalition goals: 100,000 homes a year (30% social rent); tighter asylum; nitrogen cuts (2019 Council of State ruling still blocks building). — [Max Security](https://app.max-security.com/?p=1113981), [Welingelichte Kringen](https://www.welingelichtekringen.nl/politiek/het-politieke-akkoord-van-jetten-en-bontenbal-de-hoofdpunten-en-de-hele-tekst)
- Budget Day 15 Sep 2026: "Re-earning prosperity" (welfare restraint, defence); several cuts dropped to win opposition votes; needs opposition support. — [ABN AMRO](https://www.abnamro.com/research/en/our-research/prinsjesdag-2026-a-budget-in-search-of-a-majority), [IamExpat](https://www.iamexpat.nl/expat-info/dutch-news/prinsjesdag-2026-whats-proposed-postponed-scrapped-new-budget)
- Jan 2026: seven PVV MPs led by Gidi Markuszower quit (Wilders is the party's only member), leaving the PVV with 19 seats; rebels wanted less hostile opposition. — [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2026/01/20/seven-lawmakers-quit-dutch-far-right-leader-wilders-party-in-blow-to-leadership), [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/01/dutch-far-right-pvv-hit-by-dissent-seven-mps-form-new-party/)

## Chips
- ASML: China 33% of 2025 sales; 19% in Q1 2026; expects about 20% for 2026; 2026 guidance €36–40bn; 48 EUV systems shipped in 2025; US bills (MATCH Act) target DUV sales to China. — [Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-04-16/asmls-china-sales-slump-as-export-curbs-bite-102434749.html), [Heise](https://heise.de/-10773273), [Quartz](https://qz.com/asml-2026-sales-forecast-raised-ai-chip-demand-041526)
- Nexperia (Nijmegen; owned by China's Wingtech since 2019): 30 Sep 2025, first use of the 1952 Goods Availability Act; Enterprise Chamber suspends CEO Zhang Xuezheng, puts Wingtech's shares under an administrator; China bans exports of Nexperia's China-made chips from 4 Oct, hitting carmakers; Dutch order suspended in November after talks; Feb 2026 court upholds measures and orders an investigation. — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/tech-and-telecom-law/dutch-seize-chinese-owned-chipmaker-in-first-use-of-1952-law), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-11/nexperia-feud-extended-as-court-orders-probe-into-chipmaker), [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/europe/netherlands-halts-order-on-chipmaker-nexperia/), [Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-10-13/wingtech-loses-control-of-dutch-chipmaker-nexperia-after-government-freeze-102371009.html)

## History (standard references)
- Revolt from 1568, independence recognised 1648; VOC 1602; Golden Age; kingdom 1815; WWII occupation 1940–45, more than 100,000 of 140,000 Jews murdered (Anne Frank House: about 104,000), Hunger Winter 1944–45; Indonesian war 1945–49 (2022 study: systematic extreme violence; government apology); slavery apology by Rutte (Dec 2022) and the king (Jul 2023); 1953 flood and Delta Works; Fortuyn 2002, Van Gogh 2004; MH17 2014 (196 Dutch dead); childcare-benefits scandal, government resigned Jan 2021; Groningen gas field closed. — [Britannica: Netherlands](https://www.britannica.com/place/Netherlands), [Britannica: Dutch Republic](https://www.britannica.com/place/Dutch-Republic), [Anne Frank House](https://www.annefrank.org/en/anne-frank/go-in-depth/netherlands-greatest-number-jewish-victims-western-europe/), [Wikipedia: Indonesian War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_National_Revolution), [Wikipedia: Dutch childcare benefits scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_childcare_benefits_scandal)
