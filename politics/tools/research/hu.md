May poll (Publicus): Tisza 55%, Fidesz 17%. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/13/hungarys-former-pm-orban-re-elected-party-leader-despite-election-loss), [Insight Hungary](https://insighthungary.444.hu/2026/06/19/fidesz-re-elects-orban-after-historic-loss)

## Taking the system apart
- June 2026 amendment: retroactive eight-year limit on serving as prime minister. 17th amendment, 13 Jul 2026 (139–6; 54 did not vote): ends President Sulyok's term; restores the age limit of 70 at the Constitutional Court (ending the terms of its president, Péter Polt, and three others), cuts judges' terms from 12 to 9 years, lets the Court choose its own president; shorter terms for the heads of the Kúria and the judicial office; narrows cardinal laws; creates the National Asset Recovery and Protection Office (NVVH); a 12-year (three-term) limit for MPs from the next election. — [OSW](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2026-07-14/amending-hungarys-constitution-to-dismantle-fideszs-grip-power), [Telex](https://telex.hu/english/2026/07/13/hungarian-parliament-adopts-amendment-to-fundamental-law-which-ends-term-of-orban-loyalist-president-and-sends-head-of-constitutional-court-to-retirement), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/14/hungarys-parliament-votes-to-oust-president-in-latest-anti-orban-move)
- Sulyok signed the amendment on 18 Jul under threat of impeachment, calling it a trampling of constitutional principles; his term ended 20 Jul. András Baka, the Supreme Court president whose term Orbán's government cut short in 2012, was elected president on 11 Aug (Fidesz and KDNP boycotted) and took office 19 Aug. Vote counts differ by report (140 or 146 for). — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/hungarys-president-agrees-to-step-down-amid-push-from-magyar/), [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1194331/world/europe/hungary-parliament-approves-former-judge-andras-baka-as-president), [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/international/the-former-president-of-the-hungarian-supreme-court-removed-by-orban-new-president-of-hungary_25_5821871.html)
- Public media: law dissolving MTVA in force 27 Jun 2026; new public broadcaster and news agency under an Independent Public Media Council; M1 and Kossuth news halted in early July. — [CJR](https://www.cjr.org/analysis/hungary-got-a-fresh-start-will-its-media.php), [Brussels Signal](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/07/new-hungarian-government-halts-state-television-news-broadcasts/)
- 28 Sep 2026: parliament lifts immunity of ex-ministers Miklós Seszták (bribery, ~HUF 11bn) and Balázs Hankó (National Cultural Fund grants, HUF 17.3bn); both deny wrongdoing; NVVH can take over cases and issue warrants. Fidesz calls it a "coercive machinery". — [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2026/09/29/with-two-orban-era-ministers-detained-magyars-operation-purge-takes-shape), [EUobserver](https://euobserver.com/240728/magyar-waives-his-own-immunity-as-hungary-targets-billions-in-alleged-fraud-and-two-former-orban-ministers-in-custody-day-143-of-the-tisza-government/)
- 21 Oct 2026 AP interview: accountability "must follow the rule of law"; keeps the border fence and push-backs to Serbia; plans to double child allowances and raise the minimum pension. — [AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/hungarys-magyar-vows-accountability-orbn-era-holding-firm-137437319)

## How Magyar rose
- Apr 2023 pardon of the deputy director of the Bicske children's home, convicted of helping cover up abuse; revealed Feb 2024; President Katalin Novák resigned 10 Feb 2024; Judit Varga, who countersigned as justice minister, quit public life. Magyar, Varga's ex-husband and a Fidesz insider, broke with the party that month. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katalin_Nov%C3%A1k_presidential_pardon_scandal), [Telex](https://telex.hu/english/2024/02/10/katalin-novak-has-resigned), [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/world/hungarys-president-resigns-after-pardoning-man-convicted-in-child-sexual-abuse-case)
- June 2024 European Parliament election: Tisza 29.6% and 7 seats; Fidesz–KDNP 44.6% and 11 of 21. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Parliament_election_in_Hungary), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2024/06/10/hungary-newcomer-peter-magyar-shakes-orbans-hold-on-power-early-results-say)

## History (standard references)
- 895–96 conquest under Árpád; Stephen I crowned 1000/1001; Mohács 1526; Ottoman rule of the centre to 1686–99; 1848 revolution (15 March); 1867 Compromise; Trianon 4 Jun 1920 (about two-thirds of the territory and a third of Hungarian speakers left outside); 1956 revolution (23 Oct–Nov; Nagy executed 1958, reburied 16 Jun 1989); border opened 1989; NATO 1999, EU 2004; Fidesz two-thirds in 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022; 2011 Fundamental Law; 2014 "illiberal state" speech; 2018 Article 7 vote in the European Parliament. — [Britannica: Hungary](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hungary), [Britannica: Trianon](https://www.britannica.com/event/Treaty-of-Trianon), [Britannica: 1956](https://www.britannica.com/event/Hungarian-Revolution), [Britannica: Orbán](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Viktor-Orban)
