## Reforms
- 1 July 2025: 63 provinces/cities merged into 34 (28 provinces, 6 cities); district tier abolished; NA approval 12 June 2025. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Vietnamese_administrative_reforms), [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnams-government-introduces-official-plan-for-provincial-mergers.html)
- Death penalty abolished for eight crimes incl. embezzlement and espionage (effective 1 July 2025); Truong My Lan's sentence commuted to life; SCB losses about $27bn. — [Arab News/AFP](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2605735/world)
