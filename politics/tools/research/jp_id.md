# Research note — Japan & Indonesia (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-12. Every fact in `units/jp_id.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Japan and Indonesia: occupation, romusha, PETA, Maeda's house and the 1958 reparations; aid, the 1974 Malari riots, cars, the MRT and the lost high-speed rail bid; the 2021 equipment agreement, patrol vessels, the March 2026 summit and the May 2026 Defence Cooperation Arrangement.

## Occupation and independence (`jp_id-1`)
- Mar 1942: The Dutch surrender Java to Japan
- 1942–45: Hundreds of thousands forced into labour as 'romusha'
- 1943: Japan sets up the PETA volunteer army
- 15 Aug 1945: Japan surrenders
- 17 Aug 1945: Sukarno and Hatta proclaim independence
- 1958: Peace treaty and reparations agreement
- Source: [Wikipedia: Japanese occupation of the Dutch East Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_occupation_of_the_Dutch_East_Indies)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Proclamation of Indonesian Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_of_Indonesian_Independence)
- Source: [Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan: Japan–Indonesia relations](https://www.mofa.go.jp/region/asia-paci/indonesia/)

## Aid, riots and cars (`jp_id-2`)
- 1967: Suharto's 'New Order' opens to foreign investment
- Jan 1974: Malari riots during Prime Minister Tanaka's visit
- 1977: Fukuda Doctrine promises 'heart-to-heart' ties
- 2008: Economic Partnership Agreement in force
- 2015: China wins the Jakarta–Bandung high-speed rail contract
- Mar 2019: Jakarta's first MRT line opens, financed by Japan
- Source: [Wikipedia: Malari incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malari_incident)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Fukuda Doctrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuda_Doctrine)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Jakarta MRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta_MRT)
