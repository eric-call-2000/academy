## Key facts
- Lee Jae-myung president since 4 Jun 2025 after Yoon's removal over the Dec 2024 martial law.
- Yoon sentenced to life (19 Feb 2026) for insurrection, plus 7 years (Apr 2026).
- US trade deal with $350bn investment pledge; Hyundai–LG Georgia raid (Sep 2025).
- June 2026 local elections: DP won 12 of 16 top races; Oh Se-hoon held Seoul.
- Approval ~37–38% (Gallup Korea, Sept 2026).
