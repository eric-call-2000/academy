- Trade: 20 Jul 2026 Section 338 proclamations (50% on autos, alcohol, dairy); delayed to 22 Aug; talks collapsed; Canadian counter-tariffs from 8 Sep on ~C$27.6bn; Greer 'no urgency' (25 Sep).
- Alberta referendum 19 Oct 2026 (two-option question); ~two-thirds remain (Aug 2026); Forever Canadian petition ~438–456k signatures; Carney–Smith MOU Nov 2025, Guilbeault resigned.
- Quebec election 5 Oct 2026, PQ leading (Léger ~29%); referendum after Jan 2029. Avi Lewis NDP leader 29 Mar 2026 (56%). NATO 2% met (Mar 2026); budget deficit C$78bn.
- Polls (13–26 Sep 2026): Liberals 46, Conservatives 29, NDP 15.
