## From secret contacts to the Abraham Accords (`ae_il-1`)
- 1971: UAE founded; no relations with Israel
- Jan 2010: Hamas official killed in a Dubai hotel; Dubai blames Mossad
- 2015: Israel opens an office at a UN agency in Abu Dhabi
- 13 Aug 2020: Normalisation announced; Israel suspends annexation
- 15 Sep 2020: Abraham Accords signed at the White House
- Jun 2021: Embassies open in Abu Dhabi and Tel Aviv
