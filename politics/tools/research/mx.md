# Research note — Mexico (unit 25)

Checked 2026-09-29. Every fact in `units/mx.js` and the Mexico diagram traces here.

## Key facts
- Sheinbaum elected 2 Jun 2024 (~60%); two-thirds in Congress with PVEM and PT. Approval ~68–70% (El Financiero, Aug 2026).
- Judicial election 1 Jun 2025: turnout ~13%; Morena-backed candidates won all nine Supreme Court seats; new court 1 Sep 2025.
- El Mencho killed 22 Feb 2026 in Tapalpa; 252 roadblocks in 20 states; 25 National Guard killed; 23 escaped Puerto Vallarta prison.
- 92 prisoners sent to the US in three transfers (Feb 2025–Jan 2026). Homicides down ~53% Sep 2024–Aug 2026 per government.
- Electoral reform defeated 11 Mar 2026 (259–234); 'Plan B' followed. USMCA review: fourth round Sep 2026; US demands down from 54 to 14; 50% US content in autos the sticking point.
- Gen Z marches 15 Nov 2025 after Carlos Manzo's murder (1 Nov 2025).
