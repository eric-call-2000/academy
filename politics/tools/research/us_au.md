# Research note — United States & Australia (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/us_au.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and Australia: Curtin's turn to America, ANZUS, Vietnam and Pine Gap; 9/11, Darwin rotations and AUKUS; Trump's tariffs, the AUKUS review and the October 2025 minerals deal.

## Australia looks to America (`us_au-1`)
- 27 Dec 1941: Curtin: 'Australia looks to America'
- May 1942: Battle of the Coral Sea
- 1 Sep 1951: ANZUS treaty signed with the US and New Zealand
- 1962–72: About 52,000 Australians serve in Vietnam; 521 are killed
- 1966: Treaty to set up the Pine Gap base
- 1970: Pine Gap begins operating

## From 9/11 to AUKUS (`us_au-2`)
- 14 Sep 2001: Howard invokes ANZUS for the first time after 9/11
- 2003: Australian forces join the invasion of Iraq
- 2012: US Marines begin rotations through Darwin
- 2019: Marine rotation reaches 2,500
- 15 Sep 2021: AUKUS announced; French submarine deal cancelled
- Mar 2023: Plan: US Virginia-class subs from 2032, then an AUKUS design
- Source: [AustralianPolitics.com: Howard Government Invokes ANZUS Treaty](https://australianpolitics.com/2001/09/14/howard-government-invokes-anzus-treaty.html/)
- Source: [US Indo-Pacific Command: U.S. Marines Reach 2,500 in Darwin for First Time](https://www.pacom.mil/Media/NEWS/Spotlight/Article/1918439/us-marines-reach-2500-in-darwin-for-first-time/)
