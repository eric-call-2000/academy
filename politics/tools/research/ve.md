# Research note — Venezuela (unit 28)

Checked 2026-09-29. Every fact in `units/ve.js` and the Venezuela diagram traces here.

## Key facts
- Maduro captured 3 Jan 2026 (Operation Absolute Resolve); Venezuela says ~100 killed incl. 32 Cubans; 7 US troops injured. Trial set 1 Jun 2027 (Judge Hellerstein); next hearing 17 Nov 2026.
- Delcy Rodríguez acting president since 5 Jan 2026; Cabello interior; González López defence (Mar 2026); Padrino López to agriculture.
- Boat strikes since 2 Sep 2025: at least 234 killed in ~78 strikes (to 19 Sep 2026).
- Oil: >500k bpd to US of ~1.25m bpd (Aug 2026); Chevron $7bn, double output by 2031.
- Amnesty law 19 Feb 2026; Foro Penal: 436 releases since 8 Jan plus 109 under the law; >570 still held (late Feb).
- Elections promised at UNGA 23 Sep 2026, no date; Machado's movement proposes 24 Jul 2027.
