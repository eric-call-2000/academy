## Gas (briefing 2)
- Transit 2024 ~15 bcm (14.3–15.4 depending on source); 2019 contract: 65 bcm in 2020, 40 bcm/yr 2021–24, ~$1.27bn/yr; ended 1 Jan 2025. — [Bruegel](https://www.bruegel.org/analysis/end-russian-gas-transit-ukraine-and-options-eu), [OIES](https://www.oxfordenergy.org/publications/the-end-of-russian-gas-transit-via-ukraine-immediate-impact-and-implications-for-the-european-gas-market-in-2025/), [Carnegie](https://carnegieendowment.org/russia-eurasia/politika/2025/01/russia-ukraine-europe-gas-transit)
- Cut-offs: 1 Jan 2006 (days); 7–20 Jan 2009 (13 days), EU lost ~20% of supply. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Russia%E2%80%93Ukraine_gas_dispute)
- Nord Stream 1 opened 2011; NS2 completed 2021, never operated; both damaged 26 Sep 2022. Kharkiv accords Apr 2010: fleet lease to 2042 for a gas discount.
- EU phase-out of Russian gas imports by 2027 (agreed 2025).
