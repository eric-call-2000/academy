# Research note — Saudi Arabia & China (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-01. Every fact in `units/sa_cn.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Saudi Arabia and China: the secret CSS-2 missiles and 1990 recognition; oil, Xi's 2022 visit and the 2023 Beijing deal with Iran; the 2026 Iran war, Hormuz and the yuan. The US bargain is in us_sa.

## Missiles in the desert (`sa_cn-1`)
- 1949–90: Saudi Arabia recognises Taiwan, not Beijing
- 1986: Secret deal to buy Chinese CSS-2 missiles
- 1987–88: Missiles delivered, hidden among arms bought for Iraq
- Mar 1988: The US confronts Riyadh over the missiles
- Jul 1990: Saudi Arabia and China establish relations
- 1999: Jiang Zemin visits; a strategic oil partnership begins

- Mar 2023: Saudi–Iran deal announced in Beijing
- 2023: Currency swap of about $7 billion
- 2023: Saudi Arabia invited to join BRICS
