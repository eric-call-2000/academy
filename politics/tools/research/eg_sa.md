# Research note — Egypt & Saudi Arabia (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/eg_sa.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Egypt and Saudi Arabia: Nasser's proxy war in Yemen, 1973 and the Camp David break; Gulf money after 2013 and Tiran and Sanafir; deposits, the power link and Sudan in 2026.

## Nasser against the kings (`eg_sa-1`)
- 1962: Coup in North Yemen; Egypt backs republicans, Saudi Arabia the royalists
- 1965: Around 60,000 Egyptian troops in Yemen
- Aug 1967: Khartoum deal: Egypt agrees to withdraw
- Oct 1973: Saudi oil embargo backs Egypt's war on Israel
- 1979: Saudi Arabia cuts ties over Egypt's peace with Israel
- 1987: Relations restored
- Source: [Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training: The Proxy of My Proxy: Saudi Arabia vs. Egypt in North Yemen](https://adst.org/2015/07/the-proxy-of-my-proxy-saudi-arabia-against-egypt-in-north-yemen/)

- Source: [Chatham House: Egypt and the Gulf: Sisi's debt to his Gulf Arab backers](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2020/04/egypt-and-gulf/sisis-debt-his-gulf-arab-backers)

## Billions for Sisi, islands for Riyadh (`eg_sa-2`)
- Jul 2013: Morsi ousted; Saudi Arabia, UAE and Kuwait pledge $12 billion
- 2015: Egypt joins the Saudi-led coalition in Yemen but sends no ground troops
- Apr 2016: King Salman in Cairo; islands deal and aid announced
- Oct 2016: Aramco suspends fuel shipments after a UN vote
- Jan 2017: Egypt's top administrative court voids the islands deal
- Jun 2017: Parliament approves the transfer; Sisi ratifies it
