## Steel (briefing 1)
- China crude steel **960.8 Mt** in 2025 (−4.4%), **~52%** of world output (**1,850 Mt**). — [worldsteel, Jan 2026](https://worldsteel.org/media/press-releases/2026/december-2025-crude-steel-production-2025-global-crude-steel-production/)
- China steel exports **133.6 Mt** in 2025 (worldsteel basis, incl. semi-finished; Chinese customs' finished-steel figure is lower). Record; the previous peak was ~112 Mt in 2015. — [World Steel in Figures 2026](https://worldsteel.org/data/world-steel-in-figures/world-steel-in-figures-2026/)
- US crude steel ~80 Mt (2024: 79.5 Mt). Re-check the 2025 figure at refresh.
- China ~15% of world steel in 2000 (129 Mt of ~850 Mt).
- 2016 cold-rolled flat steel from China: dumping margin **266%**, subsidy rate **256%** (final, May 2016); combined duties above 500%. — [ITA fact sheet](https://enforcement.trade.gov/download/factsheets/factsheet-multiple-cold-rolled-steel-flat-products-ad-cvd-final-051716.pdf), [IndustryWeek](https://www.industryweek.com/the-economy/competitiveness/article/21972853/us-affirms-266-dumping-margin-on-cold-rolled-steel-imports-from-china)
- US orders on Chinese steel: **34 AD + 28 CVD = 62**. — [AISI, Sept 2024](https://www.steel.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/AISI-20224-Comments-on-China-Compliance-with-WTO-Obligations-09102024-Final.docx.pdf)
- China ~3% and Vietnam ~4% of US steel imports by value (CRS); Vietnam became China's top steel export market in 2023. — [CRS IN12519](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12519), [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/article-Vietnam-should-not-become-a-release-valve-for-pressure-on-China)
- China shock: ~985,000 manufacturing jobs, up to 2.4m jobs overall, 1999–2011. — [Autor, Dorn & Hanson 2016](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-economics-080315-015041)

## Tariffs (briefing 2)
- Bush safeguard tariffs 8–30%, 5 Mar 2002 (effective 20 Mar); WTO Appellate Body against them 10 Nov 2003; lifted 4 Dec 2003. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_United_States_steel_tariff), [ASIL](https://www.asil.org/insights/volume/8/issue/26/wto-rules-against-us-safeguard-measures-steel)
- Section 232: 25% steel / 10% aluminium effective 23 Mar 2018; Canada/Mexico exempted May 2019; TRQs for EU (2022), Japan (2022), UK (2022); country exemptions ended 12 Mar 2025; 50% from 4 Jun 2025 (UK 25%); 407 derivative categories added Aug 2025. — [CRS IN12519](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12519), [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-increases-section-232-tariffs-on-steel-and-aluminum/)
- Biden, May 2024: Section 301 on Chinese steel and aluminium raised to 25% (from 0–7.5%).
- USITC 2023 (Pub 5405): covered steel imports −24%, prices +2.4%, US steel output +$1.3bn (2021); downstream prices +0.2%, output −$3.5bn (2021). — [USITC](https://www.usitc.gov/press_room/news_release/2023/er0315_63679.htm)
- Nippon Steel completed the **$14.9bn** US Steel purchase **18 Jun 2025**; golden share (presidential consent for closures, HQ moves, production transfers); ~$11bn investment by 2028. — [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/nippon-steel-finalizes-15-billion-buyout-of-u-s-steel-after-sealing-national-security-agreement), [CRS R48872](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48872)
- Supreme Court IEEPA ruling 20 Feb 2026 did not cover Section 232 (see `us.md`).

## Soybeans (briefing 3)
- US soybean exports to China **$12.2bn** in 2017 (57% of US soybean exports), **$3.1bn** in 2018; China's 25% tariff effective 6 Jul 2018. — [USITC executive briefing](https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/executive_briefings/chinasoyebot.pdf)
- Market Facilitation Program 2018–19: ~$23bn (2018 ~$8.6bn, 2019 ~$14.5bn), all commodities. — [CRS R48548](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48548/R48548.3.pdf)
- Phase One (Jan 2020): $200bn extra purchases over 2020–21; China fell well short (PIIE put it at under 60%).
- 2025: no Chinese purchases of new-crop US soybeans from spring until the Busan truce (30 Oct 2025); pledge of 12 MMT (met by Jan 2026) and ≥25 MMT/year 2026–28. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/01/21/china-buys-all-12-million-tons-of-soybeans-it-promised-just-in-time-for-trump-to-announce-new-tariffs/)
- Farmer Bridge Assistance: **$12bn** announced 8 Dec 2025 ($11bn row crops, $1bn specialty). — [USDA](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/12/08/trump-administration-announces-12-billion-farmer-bridge-payments-american-farmers-impacted-unfair)
- ASA: **15.5 MMT** ordered by 11 Sep 2026 (vs 3.7 MMT a year earlier); China keeps a **10%** retaliatory duty on US soybeans; late-Sept 2026 package of **$60bn** reciprocal tariff cuts on farm goods excludes soybeans. — [ASA via Oklahoma Farm Report](https://www.oklahomafarmreport.com/2026/09/28/asa-says-china-soybean-commitments-are-critical-amid-continued-tariffs/), [farmdoc policy news](https://farmpolicynews.illinois.edu/2026/09/china-to-slash-tariffs-on-us-ag-products-excluding-soybeans/)
- China imports ~100 Mt of soybeans a year (105 Mt in 2024); Brazil supplies most.
