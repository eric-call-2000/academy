# Research note — United States & North Korea (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-07. Every fact in `units/us_kp.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and North Korea: division, the Korean War and armistice, the Pueblo and the 1976 axe murders; the Agreed Framework, six-party talks, strategic patience and Otto Warmbier; fire and fury, Singapore, Hanoi, the DMZ and Trump's 2026 outreach.

## A war that never ended (`us_kp-1`)
- Aug 1945: US and Soviet forces divide Korea at the 38th parallel
- 25 Jun 1950: North Korea invades the South
- 27 Jul 1953: Armistice signed at Panmunjom
- 23 Jan 1968: North Korea seizes the spy ship USS Pueblo
- Dec 1968: Pueblo's crew freed after 11 months
- 18 Aug 1976: Two US officers killed in the 'axe murder' incident
- Source: [History.com: USS Pueblo captured](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/january-23/uss-pueblo-captured)
- Source: [Wilson Center Digital Archive: The USS Pueblo Incident](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/essays/uss-pueblo-incident)
- Source: [Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training: An Axe Murder Triggers a Standoff in Korea's DMZ, 1976](https://adst.org/2014/12/the-bizarre-north-korean-axe-murders/)

- Sep 2005: Six-party talks agree a joint statement
- Oct 2006: North Korea's first nuclear test
- Jun 2017: Otto Warmbier dies after release from North Korean custody
