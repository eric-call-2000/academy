# Research note — United Kingdom & Australia (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-11. Every fact in `units/gb_au.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The UK and Australia: convicts, federation, Gallipoli and Singapore; Britain's turn to Europe, the Whitlam dismissal, the Australia Act and the 1999 referendum; the free trade agreement, AUKUS and the Geelong Treaty.

## Convicts, Gallipoli and Singapore (`gb_au-1`)
- 1788: The First Fleet founds a penal colony at Sydney
- 1901: The colonies federate as the Commonwealth of Australia
- 25 Apr 1915: Anzacs land at Gallipoli
- 1931: Statute of Westminster grants full self-government
- Dec 1941: Curtin: Australia 'looks to America'
- 15 Feb 1942: Singapore falls to Japan
- Source: [Anzac Portal, Department of Veterans' Affairs: Gallipoli Campaign 1915](https://anzacportal.dva.gov.au/wars-and-missions/ww1/where-australians-served/gallipoli)
- Source: [Anzac Portal, Department of Veterans' Affairs: Fall of Singapore 1942](https://anzacportal.dva.gov.au/wars-and-missions/ww2/where/asia/singapore-1942)
- Source: [National Museum of Australia: Fall of Singapore](https://www.nma.gov.au/defining-moments/resources/fall-of-singapore)

## Europe, the dismissal and the republic (`gb_au-2`)
- 1 Jan 1973: Britain joins the European Economic Community
- 11 Nov 1975: Governor-General Kerr dismisses Whitlam
- 1986: Australia Act ends final legal ties to Westminster
- 6 Nov 1999: Republic referendum defeated, 54.9% to 45.1%
- 2022: Albanese appoints an assistant minister for the republic
- Oct 2024: King Charles visits Australia
- Source: [Australian Electoral Commission: 1999 Referendum Report and Statistics](https://www.aec.gov.au/elections/referendums/1999_referendum_reports_statistics/summary_republic.htm)
- Source: [Museum of Australian Democracy: 1999: Republic Referendum](https://moadoph.gov.au/explore/democracy/1999-republic-referendum)
- Source: [Wikipedia: 1999 Australian republic referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Australian_republic_referendum)

## A trade deal, AUKUS and a 50-year treaty (`gb_au-3`)
- 15 Sep 2021: AUKUS announced
- Dec 2021: UK–Australia free trade agreement signed
- Mar 2023: SSN-AUKUS submarine plan unveiled
- 31 May 2023: Free trade agreement takes effect
- 26 Jul 2025: Geelong Treaty signed for 50 years
- 2025–26: US reviews AUKUS, then reaffirms it
