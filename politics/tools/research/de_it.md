# Research note — Germany & Italy (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-11. Every fact in `units/de_it.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Germany and Italy: late unification, the Axis, occupation and the 1955 guest-worker agreement; the euro, the 2011 spread crisis, Monti and the recovery fund; Merz and Meloni's January 2026 Rome agreements, migration and industry.

## Unification, the Axis and guest workers (`de_it-1`)
- 1861 / 1871: Italy and Germany are unified
- 1936–39: Rome–Berlin Axis and the Pact of Steel
- Sep 1943: Italy surrenders; Germany occupies the north
- Dec 1955: Recruitment agreement brings Italian workers to Germany
- 1957: Both found the European Economic Community
- 1960s: Hundreds of thousands of Italians work in Germany
- Source: [German History in Documents and Images: Worker Treaty Rome](https://ghdi.ghi-dc.org/document.cfm?document_id=4536)
- Source: [German History in Documents: Italian Guest Workers (1962)](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/two-germanies-1961-1989/italian-guest-workers-1962)
- Source: [Historisches Lexikon Bayerns: Gastarbeiter (guest workers)](https://www.historisches-lexikon-bayerns.de/Lexikon/EN:Gastarbeiter_(guest_workers))

## The euro, 'the spread' and austerity (`de_it-2`)
- 1999: The euro is launched
- Aug 2011: ECB letter demands Italian reforms
- Oct 2011: Merkel and Sarkozy press Berlusconi
- Nov 2011: Spread hits about 575 points; Berlusconi resigns; Monti appointed
- Jul 2012: Draghi: the ECB will do 'whatever it takes'
- Jul 2020: EU agrees a joint recovery fund; Italy the biggest recipient
- Source: [LSE Euro Crisis in the Press: Italy on the brink: the hidden story of the 2011 near-collapse](https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/eurocrisispress/2013/07/29/italy-on-the-brink-the-hidden-story-of-the-2011-near-collapse-and-analogies-with-today/)
- Source: [Bloomberg: Berlusconi Bravado Proved No Match for Euro-Region Debt Crisis](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2011-11-13/berlusconi-bravado-proved-no-match-for-euro-region-debt-crisis)
- Source: [FIRSTonline: 2011 will remain in everyone's memory as the year of the euro spread](https://www.firstonline.info/en/il-2011-restera-nella-memoria-di-tutti-come-lanno-dello-spread-e-del-calvario-delleuro/)
