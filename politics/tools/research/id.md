# Research note — Indonesia (unit 22)

Checked 2026-09-29. Every fact in `units/id.js` and the Indonesia diagram traces here.

## Key facts
- Prabowo Subianto president since Oct 2024; Gibran vice-president.
- TNI law revision (March 2025) widened military roles.
- Free nutritious meals programme; Danantara sovereign fund.
- August 2025 protests over MPs' allowances and a delivery driver's death.
- Suahasil Nazara finance minister from 14 Sep 2026.
