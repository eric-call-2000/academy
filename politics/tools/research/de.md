# Research note — Germany (unit 6)

Checked 2026-09-28. Every fact in `units/de.js` and the Germany diagram traces here.

- 2025 election (23 Feb): CDU/CSU 28.5% (208), AfD 20.8% (152), SPD 16.4% (120), Greens 85 seats, Linke 8.8%; FDP and BSW (4.98%) below 5%. Merz failed the first chancellor ballot on 6 May 2025, won the second. Coalition 328 of 630. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_German_federal_election)
- Scholz coalition collapsed Nov 2024 over the budget and debt brake. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_German_government_crisis)
- March 2025 constitutional change: defence above 1% of GDP exempt from the debt brake; €500bn infrastructure fund over 12 years. — [Deutsche Bank](https://flow.db.com/topics/macro-and-markets/germany-update-breaking-from-the-brake), [OSW](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-05/germany-2026-budget-and-rising-debt)
- Defence 2026 ≈ €108.2bn (incl. special fund); 3.5% of GDP by 2029. — [Overt Defense](https://www.overtdefense.com/2025/08/11/germanys-e108-2%E2%80%AFbillion-2026-defense-budget-setting-stage-for-historic-military-build-up/), [IISS](https://www.iiss.org/publications/strategic-dossiers/the-defence-of-europe-in-a-new-era-an-assessment/capability-vignette-germanys-growing-defence-budget/)
- Military service law passed 5 Dec 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026: questionnaire and medical, service voluntary; troop goals 260–270k active, up to 470k incl. reserves by 2035; travel-permission row. — [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2025/12/05/german-parliament-approves-conscription-scheme-to-boost-the-bundeswehr/), [deutschland.de](https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/politics/new-military-service-bundeswehr), [EUobserver](https://euobserver.com/210380/germany-army-draft-battle-ready-men-travel-abroad/)
- Growth −0.3% (2023), −0.2% (2024), +0.3% (2025); 2026 forecasts 0.6% (European Commission) to 1.4% (Goldman Sachs). — [European Commission](https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/economic-surveillance-eu-member-states/country-pages-including-country-reports/germany/economic-forecast-germany_en), [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/germanys-economy-is-forecast-to-outperform-in-2026)
- July 2026: Spahn resigned (surrogacy row); Thorsten Frei elected Union group leader 170/185; reshuffle; talk of replacing Merz. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Merz_government_crisis), [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-07-29/pressure-mounts-on-germanys-merz-after-fractious-reshuffle)
- State elections: Saxony-Anhalt 6 Sep (AfD 43.8%, CDU 17.2%, no CDU constituencies); Mecklenburg-Vorpommern 20 Sep (AfD ≈38%, CDU 4.9%, out); Berlin 20 Sep (Linke 25.7%, CDU 18.8%, AfD 16.3%, Greens 14.3%, SPD 12.1%). — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/afd-germany-economy-merz.html), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/20/europe/berlin-mecklenburg-vorpommern-election-afd-intl), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/20/two-german-states-go-to-the-polls-after-far-right-gains), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Berlin_state_election)
- Polls mid-Sep: AfD 28–29%, Union ≈20%; INSA: 14% say Merz is the right chancellor. — [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/09/24/germany-merz-authority-lost-elections-afd/), [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/in-depth-research-reports/issue-brief/can-friedrich-merzs-chancellorship-survive/)
- AfD: BfV "extremist" label suspended by Cologne court Feb 2026; ~120 MPs back a ban application. — [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-02-26/german-court-grants-injunction-to-afd-party-suspending-extremist-classification-by-spy-agency), [Brussels Signal](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/02/afd-avoids-immediate-ban-after-german-court-suspends-its-extremist-status/)
