# Research note — United States & Israel (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/us_il.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and Israel: from 1948 recognition to alliance, aid and the new MOU talks, and joint war against Iran amid shifting American opinion. The Gaza and Iran wars are in il-5 and il-6.

## Eleven minutes to a special relationship (`us_il-1`)
- 14 May 1948: Truman recognises Israel 11 minutes after its founding
- 1956–57: Eisenhower forces Israel to leave Sinai after the Suez war
- Aug 1962: Kennedy sells Hawk missiles, the first major US arms sale
- 1967: The Six-Day War makes Israel a Cold War asset
- Oct 1973: Operation Nickel Grass airlifts arms mid-war
- 1978–79: Carter brokers Camp David and the Egypt–Israel treaty

- Source: [National Library of Israel: John F. Kennedy and Israel: A Look Back](https://blog.nli.org.il/en/jfk_and_israel/)

## Aid, arms and the end of aid (`us_il-2`)
- Total since 1946: About $298 billion in 2024 dollars
- Current deal: 2016 memorandum of understanding, 2019–2028
- Per year: $3.3 billion in military financing + $500 million for missile defence
- After 7 Oct 2023: Extra wartime aid passed by Congress
- New talks: Began June 2026, on phasing aid out
- Source: [Congressional Research Service: U.S. Foreign Aid to Israel: Overview and Developments since October 7, 2023](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/RL33222)
- Source: [Congressional Research Service: Possible Changes in U.S. Military Aid to Israel: Considerations for Congress](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12695.html)
- Source: [Jewish Insider: Rubio confirms administration, Israel discussing winding down U.S. aid in next MOU](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/06/rubio-israel-u-s-memorandum-of-understanding-winding-down-aid/)
