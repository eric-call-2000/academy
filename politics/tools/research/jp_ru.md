# Research note — Japan & Russia (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/jp_ru.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Japan and Russia: 1905, the 1945 seizure of the Kurils and the 1956 declaration; Abe's courtship of Putin; sanctions, Sakhalin gas and Putin's August 2026 visit to Etorofu.

## Two wars and four islands (`jp_ru-1`)
- 1855: Treaty of Shimoda: border drawn north of Etorofu
- 1905: Japan defeats Russia; wins southern Sakhalin
- 8 Aug 1945: The Soviet Union declares war on Japan
- 18 Aug–2 Sep 1945: Soviet forces seize the Kuril Islands
- 1951: The Soviet Union refuses to sign the San Francisco peace treaty
- 1956: Joint Declaration restores relations; two islands promised after a peace treaty

- Source: [PONARS Eurasia: The Southern Kuril Islands Dispute (PONARS Eurasia Policy Memo 226)](https://www.ponarseurasia.org/wp-content/uploads/attachments/pepm_226_Gorenburg_Sept2012-1.pdf)
- Source: [The Diplomat: The Details of Abe's Proposed Peace Treaty With Russia](https://thediplomat.com/2019/03/the-details-of-abes-proposed-peace-treaty-with-russia/)

## Abe's courtship of Putin (`jp_ru-2`)
- Nov 2010: Medvedev visits Kunashiri, the first Russian leader on the islands
- 2013: Abe begins a long series of meetings with Putin
- Dec 2016: Putin visits Abe's home town, Nagato
- Nov 2018: Singapore: talks to be based on the 1956 declaration
- Jan 2019: Abe and Putin meet for the 25th time; no deal
- 2020: Russia's constitution bans giving up territory
- Source: [East Asia Forum: Abe's underperforming Russia policy faces growing political backlash](https://eastasiaforum.org/2019/03/13/abes-underperforming-russia-policy-faces-growing-political-backlash/)

- Oct 2025: Takaichi tells Trump Japan needs Sakhalin gas
