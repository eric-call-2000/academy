## Nuclear rivals (briefing 1)
- Tests: India 1974; Pokhran-II 11 & 13 May 1998; Pakistan Chagai 28 & 30 May 1998. Lahore summit Feb 1999; Kargil May–Jul 1999. — [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/question/What-was-the-Kargil-War-and-why-was-it-significant)
- SIPRI Yearbook 2026 (Jan 2026): India ~190 warheads, Pakistan ~170. — [SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/media/press-release/2026/increasing-focus-nuclear-weapons-amid-heightened-escalation-risks-new-sipri-yearbook-out-now)
- Parliament attack 13 Dec 2001 → 2001–02 standoff; Mumbai 26–29 Nov 2008, 166 killed, LeT; Uri 18 Sep 2016 → "surgical strikes" 29 Sep; Pulwama 14 Feb 2019 → Balakot 26 Feb, air battle 27 Feb; Pahalgam 22 Apr 2025 → Operation Sindoor 7–10 May 2025. — [Britannica: LeT](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Lashkar-e-Taiba)
- Nuclear-installation lists exchanged every 1 Jan since 1992 (1988 agreement); 35th exchange 1 Jan 2026. Prisoner lists 1 Jan and 1 Jul: India holds 391 civilians + 33 fishermen; Pakistan 58 civilians + 199 fishermen. — [The Nation](https://www.nation.com.pk/01-Jan-2026/pakistan-india-exchange-lists-nuclear-facilities-prisoners), [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2628023/pakistan)

## Indus Waters Treaty (briefing 2)
- Signed Karachi 19 Sep 1960 (Nehru, Ayub Khan), World Bank; eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) to India, western (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) to Pakistan, ~80% of flow; run-of-river hydro allowed. — [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/brief/fact-sheet-the-indus-waters-treaty-1960-and-the-world-bank), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/event/Indus-Waters-Treaty)
- April 1948 water cut-off; Baglihar neutral expert 2007; Kishenganga CoA partial award Feb 2013, final Dec 2013 (minimum flow 9 cumecs). — [PCA](https://pca-cpa.org/en/news/indus-waters-kishenganga-arbitration-pakistan-v-india/)
- India's modification notices Jan 2023 and Aug/Sep 2024; "in abeyance" 23 Apr 2025; PCA ruling late Aug 2026, rejected by India (in-5). — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/07/08/g-s1-73122/pakistan-india-indus-waters-treaty), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/31/india-rejects-hague-court-order-to-restore-indus-waters-pact-with-pakistan)

## A closed border (briefing 3)
- India revoked MFN and set 200% duty after Pulwama (Feb 2019); Pakistan suspended trade Aug 2019; 2025 bans on indirect trade; informal trade via Dubai (~$2.3bn in 2018 by one estimate).
- Kartarpur corridor opened 9 Nov 2019, suspended May 2025; Attari–Wagah closed Apr 2025; visas cancelled; reciprocal airspace closures.
- Cricket: no bilateral series since 2012–13; 2025 Champions Trophy (India in Dubai); Asia Cup final 28 Sep 2025 (India won; no handshakes; refused trophy from Mohsin Naqvi). India's 2004 tour of Pakistan. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Asia_Cup_final), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/parasjan/2025/09/29/asia-cup-2025-india-refuses-to-receive-trophy-from-pakistans-mohsin-naqvi/)
