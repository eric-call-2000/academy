## Key facts
- Milei elected Nov 2023 (56% runoff). Midterms 26 Oct 2025: LLA 40.8%, 64 deputies (bloc ~95), 13 of 24 Senate seats; Buenos Aires province loss 7 Sep 2025.
- US $20bn swap line (Bessent, Oct 2025). Andes-Atlantic Corridor 23 Sep 2026, up to $7bn US export credit.
- Inflation: 211% (2023), 117.8% (2024), 33.5% y/y and 1.7% m/m (Aug 2026). Poverty: 52.9% (H1 2024), 28.2% (H2 2025), 32.3% (H1 2026); unemployment 7.9%.
- Labour reform: Deputies 135–115 (20 Feb 2026), Senate 42–28 (27 Feb 2026).
- Approval: Zuban Córdoba 16–19 Sep 2026, 36.1% positive / 61.8% negative; Explanans first round 38.5%. Cristina Kirchner conviction upheld Jun 2025, house arrest.
