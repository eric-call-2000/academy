## Before 1979 (briefing 1)
- Periphery doctrine (Ben-Gurion); Iran's de facto recognition 1950; Mossad–SAVAK ties; Eilat–Ashkelon pipeline 1968; Iran ~70% of Israel's oil after 1975 until 1979; Project Flower (missiles, late 1970s). — [Encyclopaedia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/israel-i-relations-with-iran/), [Furlan 2022](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13537121.2022.2041304), [Bialer](https://ismi.emory.edu/documents/Readings/Bialer_Fuel_Bridge_Israeli_oil_pipline.pdf)
- Swiss Federal Tribunal, 20 May 2015: EAPC to pay ~$1.1bn; Israel refuses. — [ASIL](https://www.asil.org/blogs/swiss-court-orders-israel-pay-iran-billion-dollar-award-oil-pipeline-dispute-may-20-2015)
- Iranian Jews ~80,000–100,000 before 1979; 9,826 in the 2016 census; one reserved Majlis seat. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Jews)
- Israeli arms to Iran in the early 1980s; Iran–Contra 1985–86.

## Shadow war (briefing 2)
- Stuxnet (~2010, attributed to US–Israel); four scientists killed 2010–12; nuclear archive seized 31 Jan 2018, shown 30 Apr 2018; US JCPOA withdrawal 8 May 2018; Fakhrizadeh killed 27 Nov 2020; Natanz incidents 2020–21.
- 1 Apr 2024 Damascus consulate strike, 16 killed incl. Gen. Mohammad Reza Zahedi. 13 Apr 2024: 170 drones, 30+ cruise, 120+ ballistic missiles; ~99% intercepted. 31 Jul 2024 Haniyeh killed in Tehran; 27 Sep Nasrallah. 1 Oct 2024: ~180–200 ballistic missiles. 26 Oct 2024 Israeli strikes. — [USIP Apr](https://iranprimer.usip.org/blog/2024/apr/15/iran%E2%80%99s-unprecedented-attack-israel), [USIP Oct](https://iranprimer.usip.org/blog/2024/oct/02/explainer-iran%E2%80%99s-missile-assault-israel), [Chatham House](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2024/04/strike-irans-consulate-syria-could-be-spark-ignites-middle-east), [HoC Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10113/)

Assad fell 8 Dec 2024. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Lebanon_pager_explosions), [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12431)
