# Research note — Saudi Arabia & Pakistan (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-07. Every fact in `units/sa_pk.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Saudi Arabia and Pakistan: Pakistani troops in the kingdom, Faisal, the Afghan jihad; workers, remittances, bailouts, Sharif's exile and the 2015 Yemen vote; the 2025 defence pact, the Iran war, the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement and the October 2026 deployment.

## Soldiers and money (`sa_pk-1`)
- 1951: Treaty of friendship signed
- 1967: First formal defence cooperation agreement
- 1974: King Faisal attends the Islamic summit in Lahore
- 1979: Soviet invasion of Afghanistan; joint support for the mujahideen
- 1980s: Up to 15,000 Pakistani troops stationed in the kingdom
- 1986: Faisal Mosque opens in Islamabad

## Workers, bailouts and a 'no' over Yemen (`sa_pk-2`)

- 2000: Nawaz Sharif exiled to Saudi Arabia
- Apr 2015: Parliament votes against joining the Yemen war
- Oct 2018: $3 billion deposit and $3 billion oil facility
- 2020: Riyadh demands early repayment after a row over Kashmir
- FY 2025–26: Saudi remittances reach about $9.8 billion
