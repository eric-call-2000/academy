## Tariffs (briefing 5)
- *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump*, argued 5 Nov 2025, decided **20 Feb 2026**, **6–3**:
  IEEPA does not authorise tariffs. Majority Roberts (opinion), Sotomayor, Kagan, Gorsuch,
  Barrett, Jackson; dissent Thomas, Alito, Kavanaugh. — [opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/24-1287_4gcj.pdf), [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/LSB11398)
- Same day: Section 122 surcharge (statute allows up to 15%, 150 days). Court of
  International Trade panel ruled against it on 7 May 2026 with limited practical effect;
  it expired **24 Jul 2026** without extension. — [Global Trade Alert](https://globaltradealert.org/blog/from-ieepa-to-section-122), [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs)
- From **24 Jul 2026**: Section 301 forced-labour tariffs, **10% or 12.5%**, on **60 economies**
  (investigations opened 12 Mar, findings 2 Jun). — [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1607-ustr-finalizes-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies-additional-tariffs-of-10-percent-or-125-percent-take-effect-24-july-2026)
- Sen. Ron Wyden introduced the Congressional Trade Powers Reform Act on 22 Jul 2026. — [Z2Data](https://www.z2data.com/insights/how-the-termination-of-section-122-tariffs-impacts-your-supply-chain/)

## Venezuela and Iran (briefing 6)
- Boat strikes from Sept 2025 (Caribbean, then eastern Pacific from Oct): at least **78
  strikes, 234 killed** incl. 17 missing presumed dead, as of 19 Sep 2026. — [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/)
- **3 Jan 2026**, Operation Absolute Resolve: strikes in Caracas, Maduro and Cilia Flores
  captured, flown to New York; Trump said no US casualties; Cuba said 32 Cubans died. Trial
  scheduled **June 2027**. — [House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10452/)
- Iran: **28 Feb 2026** US–Israeli strikes, Ali Khamenei killed; Iran struck all six GCC states;
  ceasefire **8 Apr** (Pakistan-mediated); Islamabad talks 11–12 Apr failed; US naval blockade
  **13 Apr–18 Jun**; MOU signed **17 Jun**; truce collapsed **7–8 Jul** over attacks on
  shipping; blockade reimposed **14 Jul**; September: US sank five Iranian tankers, Iran
  attacked ships and fired missiles at a base in Jordan hosting US forces. — [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/global-conflict-tracker/conflict/confrontation-between-united-states-and-iran), [CRS R45281](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R45281)
- US service members killed: **18** by July 2026 per TIME (official statements elsewhere cite
  13; the briefing attributes the figure to TIME). — [TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/07/20/us-service-members-killed-wounded-iran-war-casualties/)
- War powers: House **215–208** (3 Jun) and Senate **50–48** (23 Jun) on a resolution to end
  involvement; House **214–208** (Jul, four Republicans in favour); Senate failed **49–50**
  (24 Sep). — [NPR, 23 Jul](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/23/nx-s1-5904515/congress-iran-war-powers-vote), [NPR, 24 Sep](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/24/nx-s1-5980318/senate-iran-war-powers-vote)

## Midterms (briefing 7)
- Election **3 Nov 2026**: all 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats (22 Republican-held, incl.
  specials in Florida and Ohio); Democrats need net **+4** in the Senate. 36 states elect
  governors. — [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-history-tells-us-about-the-2026-midterm-elections/)
- Approval high 30s (36% lowest reading; 38.8% average) and generic ballot Democrats
  +5.4 (Brookings) to about +7.4 (late-September average). — [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/gop-midterm-prospects-darken-as-trump-approval-falls/), [US Polling Data](https://uspollingdata.com/news/generic-ballot-democrats-lead-widens-september-2026/)
- Economy voters' top concern. — [Pew, 23 Jul 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/politics/2026/07/23/as-the-2026-midterms-approach-economy-is-front-and-center/)
- President's party lost House seats in every midterm since 1946 except 1998 and 2002.
- Shutdown **1 Oct–12 Nov 2025**, **43 days**, longest ever; ACA subsidies were the sticking
  point. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/11/12/g-s1-97607/house-vote-shutdown-end)
- Redistricting: Texas (Aug 2025, up to +5 R), California Prop 50 (up to +5 D), Missouri,
  North Carolina, Ohio for Republicans; others on both sides. — [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Redistricting_ahead_of_the_2026_elections)
- *Louisiana v. Callais*, **29 Apr 2026**, **6–3** (Alito): second majority-Black district an
  unconstitutional racial gerrymander; Section 2 of the VRA narrowed. — [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/LSB11431)
