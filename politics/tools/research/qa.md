## The Iran war and Ras Laffan
- War began 28 Feb 2026 with US–Israeli strikes on Iran; Iran fired at Gulf states. 3 Mar: a missile hit Al Udeid, no casualties (Qatar MoD). Qatar halted LNG output on 2 Mar after drone strikes on Ras Laffan and Mesaieed. 18–19 Mar: missiles hit LNG trains 4 and 6 (12.8 mtpa, about 17% of export capacity) and a gas-to-liquids plant; force majeure on contracts with China, Italy, Belgium and South Korea; repairs 3–5 years; ~$20bn a year in lost revenue; two "cold boxes" destroyed (Reuters, 15 Oct). Government revenue −42% in H1 2026. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/19/iran-attack-qatar-lng-capacity.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/18/iran-war-qatar-ras-laffan-natural-gas-lng.html), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/iranian-strikes-cut-17-qatar-lng-output-threatening-global-supply), [Wikipedia: 2026 Iranian strikes on Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iranian_strikes_on_Qatar), [ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com/geopolitical/qatars-government-revenue-plunges-42-first-half-iran-war-knocks-out-gas-exports)
- Hormuz: LNG shipments halted from late Feb; a first transit in May after about 70 days; halts again in June–July after a tanker was attacked; transits resuming in autumn. QatarEnergy buying US LNG; reportedly negotiating US supply to 2031. 19 Oct 2026: Qatar's energy minister met Iran's acting oil minister in Doha. — [bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/qatar-resumes-lng-shipments-through-strait-of-hormuz-after-70-day-wartime-halt-441877/), [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/energy/qatari-lng-tankers-resume-hormuz-transits-despite-tensions/), [World Oil](https://worldoil.com/news/2026/6/30/shell-sees-flat-lng-trade-in-2026-as-hormuz-recovery-remains-uncertain), [Oil & Gas Middle East](https://www.oilandgasmiddleeast.com/news/ras-laffan-qatar-iran-talks)
- June 2025: Iran fired missiles at Al Udeid after US strikes on its nuclear sites; Qatar intercepted most; base evacuated beforehand; no casualties. — [Wikipedia: Iranian strikes on Al Udeid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_strikes_on_Al_Udeid_Air_Base)
- North Field: expansion from 77 to 142 mtpa by 2030 (North Field West announced Feb 2024). — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/2/25/qatar-announces-new-gas-output-boost-with-mega-field-expansion), [Gulf Times](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/677900/business/qatar-announces-raising-lng-production-capacity-to-142mn-tpy-by-2030)

## Mediation and the US
- 9 Sep 2025: Israeli strike on a Hamas compound in Doha during talks on a US plan; six killed, including a Qatari security officer and Khalil al-Hayya's son; senior leaders survived; Qatar's PM called it "state terrorism" and paused mediation. Netanyahu later apologised by phone from the White House. 29 Sep 2025 executive order: the US will "guarantee the security and territorial integrity of the State of Qatar against external attack". — [Wikipedia: Israeli attack on Doha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_attack_on_Doha), [ADN/AP](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2025/09/10/israel-threatens-enemies-everywhere-after-strike-against-hamas-in-qatar/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2025/10/1/new-trump-executive-order-guarantees-qatar-security-after-israeli-attack), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/10/01/trump-qatar-security-guarantee-israel-attack)
- Oct 2025 Gaza ceasefire: Egypt, Qatar and Turkey guarantors alongside the US; Jan 2026 Board of Peace with Qatar on its Gaza Executive Board; Aug 2026 Turkey, Egypt and Qatar condemn renewed Israeli strikes. Nov 2024: Qatar had paused mediation. — [J Street](https://jstreet.org/securing-the-ceasefire-the-roles-of-egypt-qatar-and-turkey-as-guarantors/), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/seven-nations-unite-to-join-trump-led-board-of-peace-in-push-to-end-humanitarian-crisis-1.500416367), [FDD](https://www.fdd.org/analysis/2026/01/22/trump-administration-should-be-wary-of-granting-qatar-and-turkey-executive-power-in-gaza/), [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkiye-egypt-and-qatar-demand-gaza-ceasefire-compliance-3722130), [Gulf News 2024](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/qatar/qatar-temporarily-halts-mediation-efforts-in-gaza-denies-withdrawing-completely-1.1731180033448)

## History and system (standard references)
- Al Thani rule from the 1800s; 1868 British agreement; 1916 protectorate; pearling collapse in the 1930s; oil from 1939–49; independence 1971; North Field found 1971; 1995 Hamad's palace coup; Al Jazeera 1996; first LNG 1997; Tamim emir since June 2013; 2004 constitution; Shura Council: 2021 elections for 30 of 45 seats, scrapped by referendum in Nov 2024; 2017–21 blockade by Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Egypt (13 demands; ended at AlUla, Jan 2021); 2022 World Cup; labour reforms (2020 minimum wage, end of exit permits); Taliban office from 2013, US–Taliban Doha agreement Feb 2020; Al Udeid, about 10,000 US personnel and CENTCOM's forward HQ; May 2025 gift of a Boeing 747 to the US Air Force for presidential use. — [Britannica: Qatar](https://www.britannica.com/place/Qatar), [Wikipedia: Qatar diplomatic crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_diplomatic_crisis), [Wikipedia: 2024 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Qatari_constitutional_referendum), [Amnesty](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2023/11/qatar-world-cup-one-year-on/), [Wikipedia: Doha Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doha_Agreement_(2020)), [Wikipedia: Al Jazeera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Jazeera)
