# Research note — Japan & North Korea (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-07. Every fact in `units/jp_kp.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Japan and North Korea: colonial rule, Koreans in Japan, Chongryon and the 1959–84 repatriation; the abductions, Koizumi's 2002 summit and the Stockholm agreement; missile overflights, counterstrike capability and Takaichi's summit push.

## Colony, exiles and a 'paradise' (`jp_kp-1`)
- 1910: Japan annexes Korea
- 1945: Japan's defeat ends colonial rule; Korea is divided
- 1955: Chongryon, the pro-North association of Koreans in Japan, is founded
- Dec 1959: First repatriation ship sails from Niigata
- 1959–84: About 93,000 people move to North Korea
- 1965: Japan recognises South Korea, not the North
- Source: [National Committee on North Korea: An Overview of North Korea-Japan Relations](https://www.ncnk.org/resources/briefing-papers/all-briefing-papers/overview-north-korea-japan-relations)
- Source: [History News Network: Japan's Hidden Role in the 'Return' of Zainichi Koreans to North Korea](https://www.hnn.us/article/japans-hidden-role-in-the-return-of-zainichi-korea)
- Source: [International Institute for Asian Studies: Japanese Wives in North Korea](https://www.iias.asia/the-newsletter/article/japanese-wives-north-korea)

## The abductions (`jp_kp-2`)
- 1977: Megumi Yokota, 13, kidnapped walking home in Niigata
- 1977–83: At least 17 Japanese abducted, Japan says
- 17 Sep 2002: Koizumi in Pyongyang; Kim Jong Il admits abductions
- Oct 2002: Five abductees return to Japan
- 2004: Remains said to be Megumi's fail Japanese DNA tests
- 2014: Stockholm agreement on a new investigation collapses
- Source: [Congressional Research Service (EveryCRSReport): North Korea's Abduction of Japanese Citizens and the Six-Party Talks](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/RS22845.html)
- Source: [Comparative Connections (Pacific Forum): Mr. Koizumi Goes to Pyongyang](https://cc.pacforum.org/2002/10/mr-koizumi-goes-pyongyang/)
- Source: [The Japan Times: Time running out for families of Japanese abducted by North Korea](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2022/10/14/national/north-korea-abductees-time-running-out/)

## Missiles overhead and a summit offer (`jp_kp-3`)
- Aug 1998: A Taepodong rocket flies over Japan
- Aug–Sep 2017: Two missiles over Hokkaido; J-Alert sirens
- 4 Oct 2022: First missile over Japan in five years
- Dec 2022: Japan decides to acquire counterstrike missiles
- Mar 2026: Kim's sister rejects a summit with Takaichi
- Sep 2026: Takaichi asks Trump to help broker a meeting
