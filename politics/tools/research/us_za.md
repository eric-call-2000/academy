# Research note — United States & South Africa (relationship)

Checked 2026-10-07. Every fact in `units/us_za.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: The US and South Africa: Cold War ties, constructive engagement, the anti-apartheid movement and the 1986 veto override, Mandela's watch-list status; AGOA, PEPFAR, BRICS, Lady R and the ICJ case; aid cuts, Afrikaner refugees, tariffs, the end of HIV aid and the September 2026 visa restrictions.

## Apartheid and sanctions (`us_za-1`)
- 1948: South Africa's National Party introduces apartheid
- 1960s–70s: US sees Pretoria as a Cold War bulwark
- 1981: Reagan adopts 'constructive engagement'
- 1984–86: Protests, divestment and arrests at the embassy in Washington
- 2 Oct 1986: Congress overrides Reagan's veto of sanctions
- 1 Jul 2008: Mandela taken off a US terrorism watch list

- Source: [Ronald Reagan Presidential Library: Statement on the Comprehensive Anti-Apartheid Act of 1986](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/archives/speech/statement-comprehensive-anti-apartheid-act-1986)
- Source: [CNN: Mandela off U.S. terrorism watch list](https://www.cnn.com/2008/WORLD/africa/07/01/mandela.watch/)

## Trade, AIDS and drift (`us_za-2`)
- Oct 1994: Mandela's first state visit to Washington
- 2000: AGOA opens US markets to African goods duty-free
- 2003: Bush launches PEPFAR to fight HIV/AIDS
- 2010: South Africa joins BRICS
- Feb 2023: Naval drills with Russia and China
- Dec 2023: South Africa takes Israel to the ICJ
