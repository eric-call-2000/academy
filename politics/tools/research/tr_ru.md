## Empires (briefing 1)
- Twelve wars 1568–1918; Crimea 1783; Crimean War 1853–56. — [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/event/Russo-Turkish-wars)
- 1921 treaties (Kars, Ardahan); Montreux 1936 — [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/event/Montreux-Convention); Soviet demands 1945–46 — [Russia in Global Affairs](https://eng.globalaffairs.ru/articles/1945-turkish-soviet-crisis/); NATO 18 Feb 1952 — [NATO](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/nato-history/history-by-theme/my-country-and-nato/tuerkiye-and-nato)
- Crimean Tatars deported 1944; Turkey did not recognise 2014 annexation.

## Crisis (briefing 2)
- Su-24 shot down 24 Nov 2015; pilot and a marine killed; sanctions; letter Jun 2016; Karlov killed 19 Dec 2016. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/parallels/2016/12/20/506278517/after-diplomats-killing-russia-doubles-down-on-ties-with-turkey), [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/new-atlanticist/turkey-russia-relationship-will-survive-russian-ambassador-s-assassination/)
- Astana process 2017; Idlib strike 27 Feb 2020 killed 34 Turkish soldiers.

## Energy and arms (briefing 3)
- Russian share of gas imports: ~60% two decades ago, 41.3% in 2024. — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/turkeysource/azerbaijan-and-us-helping-turkey-diversify-gas/)
- TurkStream opened 8 Jan 2020. Akkuyu: 4 × VVER-1200, 4,800 MW, ~$20bn, build-own-operate. — [Akkuyu](https://akkuyu.com/en/about/info)
- S-400 delivered Jul 2019; F-35 removal 2019; CAATSA Dec 2020; still unresolved Aug 2026. — [Chatham House](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2026/07/turkeys-s-400-saga-shows-hard-realities-defence-procurement)
- Russian visitors 6.9m in 2025. — [Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/business/who-visited-turkiye-most-in-2025-russia-germany-uk-take-lead-3213972)
- Erdoğan–Putin at SCO summit, Sep 2026. — [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/09/01/nuclear-akkuyu-erdogan-russia-putin/a165bf82-a618-11f1-9e38-f705d048bd5a_story.html)
