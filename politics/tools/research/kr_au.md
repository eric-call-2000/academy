Scope: South Korea and Australia: UNTCOK, the Korean War and Kapyong; iron ore, coal, gas, KAFTA, students and the 2021 partnership and K9 deal; the Redback, the lost frigate bid, Hanwha's Austal stake and the September 2026 energy summit.

## Kapyong and the Korean War (`kr_au-1`)
- 1947–48: Australia sits on the UN commission for Korean elections
- Jun 1950: North Korea invades; Australia commits forces
- Apr 1951: Battle of Kapyong
- 1953: Armistice; about 340 Australians killed in the war
- 1961: Diplomatic relations established
- 1965–73: Both send troops to the Vietnam War
- Source: [Australian War Memorial: Korean War, 1950–53](https://www.awm.gov.au/articles/event/korea)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Battle of Kapyong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kapyong)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Australia–South Korea relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia%E2%80%93South_Korea_relations)

## Iron ore, coal and KAFTA (`kr_au-2`)
- 1970s: Australian ore and coal feed POSCO's new steelworks
- 1990s: Korea becomes one of Australia's top export markets
- Apr 2014: Free trade agreement signed in Seoul
- Dec 2014: KAFTA comes into force
- 2021: Comprehensive Strategic Partnership; K9 howitzer deal
- Nov 2025: Albanese attends APEC in Gyeongju
- Source: [Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade: Korea–Australia Free Trade Agreement](https://www.dfat.gov.au/trade/agreements/in-force/kafta/korea-australia-fta)
- Source: [Asialink, University of Melbourne: Beyond BTS: a mic drop moment for Australia–Korea relations](https://asialink.unimelb.edu.au/diplomacy/insights/beyond-bts-mic-drop-moment-australia-korea-relations/)
- Source: [Wikipedia: Australia–South Korea relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia%E2%80%93South_Korea_relations)
