## Trade (briefing 1)
- Two-way goods trade 2025 **$872.8bn**, a record; Mexico the top US goods partner for the third year running (since 2023). — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-mexico-trade-hits-new-high-of-872b-in-2025), [Mexico Business News](https://mexicobusiness.news/trade-and-investment/news/mexico-remains-united-states-top-trade-partner)
- Maquiladora (Border Industrialization) programme 1965. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maquiladora)
- Parts cross borders several times (four crossings for one part in NBC's example). — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/autos/one-auto-part-crosses-border-four-way-car-rcna200706)
- USMCA review 2026; if not all three confirm, annual reviews and expiry in 2036 (see mx-7 sources). — [CRS IF11175](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF11175)
- IEEPA "fentanyl" tariffs struck down 20 Feb 2026; Section 232 tariffs remain (see us.md).

## Migration and remittances (briefing 2)
- Border Patrol southwest-border apprehensions FY2025 **237,565**, lowest since FY1970. — [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/lowest-fiscal-year-border-patrol-apprehensions-1970), [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/02/02/migrant-encounters-at-the-us-mexico-border-are-at-their-lowest-level-in-more-than-50-years/)
- Deportations of Mexicans 2025 ~**160,000** (−22% vs 2024). — [BBVA Research](https://www.bbvaresearch.com/en/publicaciones/mexico-did-deportations-of-mexicans-from-the-us-increase/)
- Remittances 2025 **$61.8bn**, −4.6%, biggest fall since 2009 (Banxico). — [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/news/remittance-biggest-decline-in-16-years/)
- 1% US excise tax on cash-funded remittances from 1 Jan 2026 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act). — [Border Report](https://www.borderreport.com/news/trade/1-percent-tax-on-remittances-from-us-takes-effect-in-2026/)
- ~11 million Mexican-born people in the US (Pew, ~10.9m in 2023). Bracero programme 1942–64. Sheinbaum sent 10,000 Guard troops to the border in Feb 2025; "México te abraza" reception programme.

## Guns and drugs (briefing 3)
- US overdose deaths 2025 ~**69,973**, −14% vs 2024 (81,313); third annual fall. — [CDC NCHS](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/pressroom/releases/20260513.html)
- ~70% of guns recovered in Mexico and traced (2014–18) were US-sourced. — [GAO-21-322](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-21-322)
- *Smith & Wesson v. Estados Unidos Mexicanos*, 5 Jun 2025, 9–0 (Kagan): PLCAA bars the suit. — [Opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-1141_lkgn.pdf)
- Transfers of cartel prisoners: 29 (28 Feb 2025, incl. Caro Quintero), 26 (12 Aug 2025), 37 (20 Jan 2026) = 92. — [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/mexico-extradites-drug-traffickers-us-rafael-caro-quintero/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/21/mexico-sends-37-more-drug-cartel-suspects-to-us-amid-trump-attack-threats)
- Feb 2025 FTO designations: six Mexican cartels among eight groups. El Mencho killed 22 Feb 2026 (mx-6).
