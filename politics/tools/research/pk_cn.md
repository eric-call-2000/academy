# Research note — Pakistan & China (relationship)

Checked 2026-09-30. Every fact in `units/pk_cn.js` traces to the sources below (cited per briefing in the unit).
Scope: Pakistan and China: the 'all-weather' friendship, CPEC under militant attack, and Chinese weapons in the May 2025 conflict. The four-day war is in pk-5.

## Iron brothers (`pk_cn-1`)
- 1951: Diplomatic relations established
- 1963: Border agreement; Pakistan cedes the Shaksgam valley claim
- 1971: Pakistan hosts Kissinger's secret trip to Beijing
- 1979: Karakoram Highway completed
- 1980s–90s: Chinese help for Pakistan's nuclear and missile programmes, according to US officials
- 2015: China–Pakistan Economic Corridor launched
- Source: [Al Jazeera: 'Iron brothers': How China and Pakistan built an unlikely 75-year bond](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/5/21/iron-brothers-how-china-and-pakistan-built-an-unlikely-75-year-bond)
- Source: [Council on Foreign Relations: China-Pakistan Relations](https://www.cfr.org/backgrounders/china-pakistan-relations)
- Source: [National Security Archive: Kissinger's Secret Trip to China](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB66/)

## The corridor under fire (`pk_cn-2`)
- Launched: 2015, during Xi Jinping's visit to Islamabad
- Pledged: About $62 billion in projects and loans
- Main projects: Power plants, motorways, Gwadar port and an airport
- Threat: Baloch separatists and the Pakistani Taliban
- Chinese deaths: At least 20 Chinese nationals killed in five years
- Source: [The Diplomat: Pakistan's New Special Security Unit Underscores China's Hold on the Country](https://thediplomat.com/2026/02/pakistans-new-special-security-unit-underscores-chinas-hold-on-the-country/)
- Source: [Stimson Center: Pakistan's Security Challenges Threaten to Undermine Its Relationship with China](https://www.stimson.org/2024/pakistans-security-challenges-threaten-to-undermine-its-relationship-with-china/)
- Source: [VOA via GlobalSecurity.org: Suicide Bombing Kills 5 Chinese Citizens in Pakistan](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/pakistan/2024/pakistan-240326-voa01.htm)
- Source: [ThePrint: Beijing's losing patience with Pakistan. Attacks on Chinese nationals raising CPEC stakes](https://theprint.in/opinion/china-pakistan-bla-attacks-cpec/2862760/)

## Chinese weapons, tested in battle (`pk_cn-3`)
- 1960s: Chinese tanks and jets begin to arrive
- 2007: First JF-17 fighters, developed jointly, enter service
- 2020–24: About 81% of Pakistan's arms imports come from China
- 2022: J-10C fighters delivered
- May 2025: J-10Cs and PL-15 missiles used against India
- 2025–26: Talks on China's J-35 stealth fighter
- Source: [Global Times: Pakistan confirms participation of J-10C jets in response to Indian attacks: report](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202505/1333573.shtml)
- Source: [SIPRI: Trends in International Arms Transfers, 2024](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/fs_2503_at_2024_0.pdf)
- Source: [Military Watch Magazine: Why Pakistan's Defence Minister Just Brought J-35 Stealth Fighter Procurement Plans Into Serious Question](https://militarywatchmagazine.com/article/pakistani-defence-minister-uncertainty-j35-plans)
