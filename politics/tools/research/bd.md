## History (standard references)
- Plassey 1757; partition of Bengal 1905/1911; 1947; Language Movement 21 Feb 1952; six points 1966; 1970 cyclone and election; Operation Searchlight 25 Mar 1971; independence 26 Mar; India enters 3 Dec; surrender 16 Dec (~93,000 POWs); 10 million refugees; deaths 300,000–3 million; Mujib killed 15 Aug 1975; Zia 1977–81; Ershad 1982–90; 1991 democracy; caretaker system 1996–2011; 2007–08 army-backed government; Rana Plaza 2013 (>1,100 dead); Rohingya exodus 2017 (~740,000); Bhasan Char (~35,000). — [Britannica: history of Bangladesh](https://www.britannica.com/topic/history-of-Bangladesh), [Britannica: Liberation War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Bangladesh-Liberation-War), [Wikipedia: July Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_Revolution_(Bangladesh)), [Wikipedia: Rohingya refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohingya_refugees_in_Bangladesh)
