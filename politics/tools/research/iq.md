## Election and government
- 11 Nov 2025 election: Sudani's Reconstruction and Development Coalition first with 46 of 329 seats; State of Law (Maliki) 29; turnout about 56%; Shia lists 187 seats, Sunni 77, Kurdish 56, minorities 9; results certified by the Federal Supreme Court. Muqtada al-Sadr's movement boycotted. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Iraqi_parliamentary_election), [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/iraqi-prime-minister-s-coalition-tops-parliamentary-elections-with-46-seats/3746560), [Washington Institute](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/iraqs-election-outcomes-and-next-steps), [Carnegie](https://carnegieendowment.org/middle-east/diwan/2025/12/toward-a-new-compromise-in-iraq)
- The Coordination Framework first backed Nouri al-Maliki (Jan 2026); Trump warned all US support would stop if he became PM; Maliki and Sudani withdrew. 11 Apr 2026: parliament elected Nizar Amidi (PUK) president, 227–15 in the run-off, about five months late; speaker Haibat al-Halbousi. 27 Apr: Amidi named Ali al-Zaidi, a businessman from Dhi Qar in his forties with no previous office (chair of the National Holding Company), PM-designate. 14 May: parliament approved his programme and 14 of 23 ministers; cabinet completed in Oct 2026 (25 ministers). — [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/28/who-is-ali-al-zaidi-the-businessman-named-as-iraqs-pm-designate), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/04/meet-ali-al-zaidi-businessman-and-iraqs-new-prime-minister-designate), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/14/iraqs-parliament-approves-new-ali-al-zaidi-government), [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/world/iraqi-premier-completes-cabinet-after-parliament-approves-11-ministers-3724491), [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/iraqs-parliament-elects-nizar-amedi-as-new-president?amp=1)

## The war, the US and the militias
- US–Israeli war on Iran from 28 Feb 2026; Iran-backed militias fired on US troops; US struck them; more than 1,000 Iranian missile and drone attacks on the Kurdistan Region since February (Rudaw). 30 Sep 2026: last US troops leave, ending the anti-IS mission under a 2024 deal. — [ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/us-completes-iraq-troop-withdrawal-ending-12-year-mission-against-isis-islamic-state-group-baghdad-kurdish-region-iran-war-attacks-military-bases-prime-minister-al-zaidi), [FDD Long War Journal](https://www.fdd.org/analysis/2026/09/30/us-forces-complete-departure-from-iraq-as-anti-islamic-state-mission-in-iraq-ends/), [Times Live/AP](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/world/2026-09-15-as-last-us-troops-pack-up-in-iraq-militias-signal-their-weapons-will-stay/)
- Disarmament: Zaidi's 30 Sep 2026 deadline extended; June 30, 2027 set for weapons outside state control; Kataib Hezbollah, Nujaba and others resist; Kataib Sayyid al-Shuhada sets conditions; PMF law draft (18 articles) puts the force under the commander-in-chief. — [Wikipedia: Disarmament of Iraqi militias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disarmament_of_Iraqi_militias), [Long War Journal](https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2026/09/iranian-backed-militia-in-iraq-sets-conditions-for-disarmament.php), [FDD](https://www.fdd.org/analysis/iraq-delays-militia-disarmament-but-doesnt-give-up-for-now/), [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/en/Iraq/Iraq-armed-groups-condition-disarmament-on-end-of-occupation)

## Oil
- Pre-war exports about 3.5m bpd from the south via Hormuz; fell to zero when Iran closed the strait. Oil revenue (at least 90% of the budget) fell from $6.8bn in February to about $2.3bn a month in May–June; July salaries delayed for many public employees. Early Aug 2026: one-year Iraq–Turkey deal for at least 750,000 bpd via Kirkuk–Ceyhan (then about 200,000 bpd by AP's count; other officials gave lower figures). — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/08/04/iraq-struggles-with-july-salary-payments-as-oil-revenue-fails-to-cover-budget/), [WTOP/AP](https://wtop.com/world/2026/08/iraq-and-turkey-strike-a-1-year-oil-pipeline-deal-to-boost-exports-during-ongoing-hormuz-closures/), [Hellenic Shipping News](https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/iraqs-oil-exports-dip-to-500000-bpd-in-may-june/)

## History (standard references)
- Mesopotamia; Abbasid Baghdad (762); Mongol sack 1258; Ottoman rule; British mandate 1920 and the 1920 revolt; Faisal I king 1921; independence 1932; 1958 revolution; Ba'ath coups 1963 and 1968; Saddam president 1979; Iran–Iraq war 1980–88; Anfal 1986–89 and Halabja (16 Mar 1988, about 5,000 killed); Kuwait 1990–91; sanctions; 2003 invasion; 2005 constitution; 2006–08 sectarian war; IS takes Mosul June 2014, Sistani's fatwa, Yazidi genocide; Mosul retaken July 2017; Kurdish referendum Sep 2017 (about 93% yes), Kirkuk retaken Oct 2017; 2019 protests (hundreds killed), PM Abdul-Mahdi resigns; Sudani PM Oct 2022. Population about 46 million (2024 census). Oil reserves about 145bn barrels, roughly the fifth-largest. — [Britannica: Iraq](https://www.britannica.com/place/Iraq), [Britannica: history of Iraq](https://www.britannica.com/topic/history-of-Iraq), [Britannica: Iraq War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Iraq-War), [Wikipedia: Halabja massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halabja_massacre), [Wikipedia: Battle of Mosul (2016–2017)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mosul_(2016%E2%80%932017))
