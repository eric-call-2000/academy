## The snap election
- 29 Sep 2026: government approves two emergency housing decrees (halt evictions of vulnerable tenants, curb speculation, tax breaks for renting; automatic lease renewal). 2 Oct: Congress rejects them, the first 178–172, with PP, Vox and Junts against. Trigger: the eviction of an 87-year-old Madrid pensioner. 3 Oct Madrid march: organisers ~500,000, authorities ~70,000. — [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/2/spains-parliament-rejects-govt-housing-decrees-amid-mass-protests), [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2026/10/03/nearly-500000-people-protest-in-madrid-over-spains-housing-crisis)
- 5 Oct 2026: Sánchez calls a general election for 29 Nov 2026, about ten months early; the 2023 budget was rolled over three times. Polls: RTVE average put PP+Vox above 200 seats; 22 Oct weighted average PP 31.8%, PSOE 26.4%, Vox 18.9%; GESOP: PP+Vox 189–197 seats, Vox 68–72 (from 33); the state CIS puts the PSOE first. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/5/spanish-prime-minister-pedro-sanchez-announces-snap-election), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/05/nx-s1-5991404/spain-snap-elections), [Brussels Signal](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/10/sanchez-dissolves-parliament-and-calls-a-november-election/), [The Spanish Eye](https://www.thespanisheye.com/2026/10/12/spain-election-poll-pp-and-vox-could-secure-big-majority-with-up-to-197-seats/), [Wikipedia: polling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadership_opinion_polling_for_the_2026_Spanish_general_election)
- The left: Yolanda Díaz said in Feb 2026 she will not be a candidate again; Sumar around 6% and Podemos (Irene Montero) around 4% in early 2026; talks on a new joint list. — [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/politics/goodbye-to-yolanda-diaz-she-will-not-be-candidate-in-the-elections-again_1_5658760.html), [Jacobin](https://jacobin.com/2026/02/spain-left-sanchez-podemos-sumar)

## Catalonia and the amnesty
- 2023 election: PP 137, PSOE 121, Vox 33, Sumar 31, ERC 7, Junts 7, Bildu 6, PNV 5; Sánchez re-invested Nov 2023 with 179 votes after agreeing an amnesty. Amnesty law in force June 2024; Constitutional Court upheld it in 2025. — [Wikipedia: 2023 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Spanish_general_election)
- Junts broke with the PSOE in late Oct 2025 (executive in Perpignan, members 87% in favour): "We are going into opposition." Seven seats. — [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/politics/junts-members-endorse-breaking-with-sanchez-with-87-support_1_5546089.html), [Catalan News](https://www.catalannews.com/politics/item/pm-sanchez-admits-relations-with-junts-are-broken-but-hopes-to-mend-them)
- 6 Oct 2026: Constitutional Court rules the amnesty covers Puigdemont, including embezzlement; Judge Llarena lifts warrants for Puigdemont, Comín and Puig; Supreme Court still has to archive the case; Puigdemont's lawyer says he will return once it applies in full; Junts says he is unlikely to stand on 29 Nov. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/6/catalan-separatist-leader-puigdemont-to-return-home-after-warrant-lifted), [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/politics/the-constitutional-court-orders-the-supreme-court-to-amnesty-the-1-leaders-in-preferential-and-urgent-manner_1_5870772.html)
- 1 Oct 2017 referendum, 27 Oct declaration, Article 155 direct rule; 2019 Supreme Court sentences (sedition) of 9–13 years; 2021 pardons; Salvador Illa (PSC) Catalan president since Aug 2024. — [Britannica: Catalonia](https://www.britannica.com/place/Catalonia), [Wikipedia: 2017 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Catalan_independence_referendum)

## Economy, migration
- Growth: IMF (Oct 2026) 2.5% in 2026; Commission (May 2026) 2.4% vs 0.9% for the euro area, unemployment 9.9%; fastest-growing major advanced economy for a third year. Debt close to 100% of GDP. — [Mallorca Daily Bulletin](https://www.majorcadailybulletin.com/news/local/2026/10/13/146559/spain-ranked-the-fastest-growing-major-advanced-economy-for-the-third-year-running.html), [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/economy/brussels-only-revises-upwards-the-forecasts-for-spain-and-places-it-as-the-locomotive-of-europe_1_5743956.html), [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/why-spains-economy-is-growing-three-times-faster-than-the-euro-area), [The Corner](https://thecorner.eu/news-spain/cge-updates-spains-gdp-growth-to-2-4-in-2026-and-points-to-public-debt-still-close-to-100/124567/)
- Migration: Jan 2026 plan to regularise about 500,000 undocumented migrants by decree; Canary Islands sea arrivals down about 60% to mid-August 2026; Ceuta land arrivals up. — [Opinio Juris](https://opiniojuris.org/2026/08/26/human-rights-at-sea-and-spains-2026-migration-paradox/), [RTHK](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1841623-20260127.htm)

## Washington, NATO, Israel
- June 2025 NATO summit: Spain exempted from the 5% target; Sánchez says 2.1% is "sufficient and realistic". SIPRI: 1.4% (2021) to 2.1% (2025). Trump tariff threats (2025), threat to end trade after Spain barred US use of joint bases (Rota, Morón) for strikes on Iran (Mar 2026), and again in July 2026 in Ankara; Rutte defended Spain; no tariffs imposed. — [OPB/AP](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/03/03/trump-threatens-to-cut-off-trade-with-spain-after-it-disallowed-us-use-of-joint-bases-in-iran-war/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/03/trump-threatens-spain-trade-war-iran-army-base), [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/trump-orders-end-us-trade-spain-after-madrid-rejects-nato-spending-target-terrible-partner-598170)

## History and system (standard references)
- 711 Muslim conquest; 1492 fall of Granada, expulsion of Jews, Columbus; Habsburg empire; 1714 fall of Barcelona (11 Sept, the Diada); 1812 Cádiz constitution; 1898 loss of Cuba and the Philippines; 1931 Second Republic; 1936–39 civil war; Franco to 1975; 1978 constitution; 23 Feb 1981 coup attempt; EC 1986; ETA's end (2011 ceasefire, 2018 dissolution); 2018 no-confidence vote brings Sánchez to power. Congress 350 seats by province (D'Hondt), investiture by Congress, decree-laws must be validated by Congress within 30 days, 17 autonomous communities. — [Britannica: Spain](https://www.britannica.com/place/Spain), [Britannica: Spanish Civil War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Spanish-Civil-War), [Wikipedia: Spanish Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Constitution_of_1978)
- Valencia floods 29 Oct 2024 (over 220 dead); Iberian blackout 28 Apr 2025. — [Wikipedia: 2024 floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Spanish_floods), [Wikipedia: 2025 blackout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Iberian_Peninsula_blackout)

## Background used in the briefings
- Judicial council unrenewed 2018–2024 until an EU-brokered PP–PSOE deal (June 2024). PSC–ERC deal (2024) for Catalonia to collect its own taxes. Puigdemont elected MEP 2019; Belgian and German courts refused extradition on the main charges; brief return to Barcelona on 8 Aug 2024. Venice Commission opinion on the amnesty (March 2024). Over-tourism protests 2024–25; Barcelona to end tourist-flat licences by 2028. 2023 housing law lets regions cap rents in "stressed" areas. Covid state of alarm partly ruled unconstitutional (2021). CIS poll (Nov 2025): about one in five rate the Franco years good or very good. — [Wikipedia: Puigdemont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carles_Puigdemont), [Wikipedia: Spanish housing law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_housing_law_2023), [Wikipedia: amnesty law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_amnesty_law_2024)
