## The 1992 Consensus (briefing 1)
- SEF (1991) and ARATS (1991); Hong Kong meeting Oct 1992; term coined by Su Chi in 2000; Koo–Wang talks Singapore 27 Apr 1993. — [Wikipedia: 1992 Consensus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Consensus), [Wang–Koo summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang%E2%80%93Koo_summit)
- Ma Ying-jeou era: 23 agreements; ECFA June 2010; Ma–Xi meeting Singapore 7 Nov 2015; Sunflower Movement Mar–Apr 2014; contact cut 2016; Xi's Jan 2019 speech linking the consensus to "one country, two systems".
- Cheng Li-wun (KMT chair since Oct 2025) met Xi in Beijing 10 Apr 2026. — [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/04/11/2003855400), [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/how-do-taiwanese-feel-about-the-cheng-xi-meeting/)

## Economic ties (briefing 2)
- 2025: US 30.9% of Taiwan's exports ($198.27bn), first time No. 1 since 1999; China + HK 26.6% ($170.48bn, +13.2%), vs 43.9% in 2020. — [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202601090021), [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/u-s-overtakes-china-as-no-1-taiwan-export-market-in-2025-1st-since-1999-ce7e5bdede8af52d)
- China's share of Taiwan's approved outbound investment: 83.8% (2010) → 3.7% (2024). — [Taiwan News](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6332968)
- ECFA suspensions: 12 items from 1 Jan 2024; 134 items from 15 Jun 2024. Pineapple ban Mar 2021; grouper ban Jun 2022; group tourism halted 2019. — [Globe and Mail/Reuters](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-china-suspends-tariff-concessions-on-134-items-under-taiwan-trade-deal/)

## Kinmen (briefing 3)
- Guningtou, Oct 1949. 1954–55 and 1958 crises; 23 Aug 1958 bombardment (~25,000+ rounds in the first hours); odd/even-day shelling to 1979; martial law on Kinmen until 1992. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Taiwan_Strait_Crisis), [Hoover](https://www.hoover.org/research/guns-august-taiwan-strait-1958)
- Mini three links from 2 Jan 2001; 20 millionth passenger 2023. Jinjiang–Kinmen water pipeline from Aug 2018. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinjiang%E2%80%93Kinmen_Pipeline)
- 14 Feb 2024 speedboat capsizing (two dead); China Coast Guard patrols from 18 Feb 2024; tourist-boat boarding; ~60 incursions reported by Mar 2025. — [AMTI](https://amti.csis.org/a-new-normal-for-the-china-coast-guard-at-kinmen-and-matsu/), [RFA](https://www.rfa.org/english/china/2025/03/24/china-taiwan-kinmen/)
